You may also turn the array: 8 rows of 7 is the same 56 marks. So **7 × 8 = 8 × 7 = 56**. That is the **commutative** fact: the groups can be swapped; the product stays the same. The word is used here for this one idea, not as a long new theory.

**Abstract:** 7 × 8 = 56, and 8 × 7 = 56. A week has 7 days. Eight weeks have 8 × 7 = 56 days. That 56 is a calendar count of days, not a NASA month. The NASA month in this level comes later in the week.

<p class="note">The length of the month used in this level is from the NASA Moon fact sheet: the Moon’s synodic period is 29.53 days, taught here as <strong>about 29½ days</strong>. https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html This level does not invent a finer official length, and does not begin the sidereal month or Moon phases as fractions (those belong later). ## Math circle: why turning an array does not change the product {#circle}

<p class="meta">20 minutes · one rich problem, solved and then explained</p>

<div class="learn" data-listen="en-US">

<h3>Learn</h3>

An array of 7 rows of 8 has the same marks as 8 rows of 7. The picture is turned; the marks are not added or taken away. That is why 7 × 8 = 8 × 7.

</div>

<div class="remember" data-listen="en-US">
