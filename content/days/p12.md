<p class="note"><strong>For the guide:</strong> Work through the blocks in order. Count in 9s: 9, 18, 27, 36. The product is **36**. You may also see 9 rows of 4: 9 × 4 = 36. 4 × 9 = 9 × 4.

Concrete: four strips of 9 cubes, or four rows of 9 counters (invented classroom marks, labeled invented). Then the picture, then 4 × 9 = 36.

A week has 7 days, so this 4-by-9 array is not a picture of four weeks. Four weeks would be 4 × 7 = 28 days. Do not mix the 9s in the array with the 7 days of a week.

<p class="note">The length of the month used in this level is from the NASA Moon fact sheet: the Moon’s synodic period is 29.53 days, taught here as <strong>about 29½ days</strong>. https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html This level does not invent a finer official length, and does not begin the sidereal month or Moon phases as fractions (those belong later). Classroom bag, rock, and array counts are invented and labeled invented. They are not official NASA counts. A week of 7 days is a calendar fact used here, not a NASA orbital period.</p>
