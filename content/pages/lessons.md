Units 1–5 cover **Level 4** (big numbers), **Level 5** (multiplication and division), **Level 6** (fractions, Moon phases, and gravity on other worlds), **Level 7** (metric measurement, perimeter, area, and volume, with a labeled Mars-habitat model), and **Level 8** (angles, polygons, circumference with a labeled π, symmetry, and a first construction) of the [level map](levels.html). Each day has mental math, a core lesson, challenge problems, a math circle, a short Spanish lesson, a short Latin lesson, and a notebook project (with writing stems and a printable worksheet set).

**Math circle topics so far:** systematic counting, logic grids, magic squares, parity, the balance puzzle, counting pairs, the pigeonhole principle, divisibility tests, sand-timer puzzles, colouring invariants. Unit 4 adds a comparing argument (is a rounded 6,800 km still honest?), a same-perimeter / different-area observation, a units argument (why a length is not an area), and a last argument about labeling invented models beside sourced facts. Unit 5 adds a reason that vertically opposite angles are equal, a triangle-count reason for (*n* − 2) × 180°, a scaling reason that C/d is the same for every circle, a first look at why an orbit can be curved (no invented speed), and a last argument about labeling π.
