# Placement check

This check is for mathematics only. Spanish and Latin are beginner courses: start them at Day 1 whatever the mathematics level.

Do this with your guide over one or two sittings. It is not a test to pass: it finds where to start. Work section by section. **Stop** after any section with two or more answers wrong, or when the work stops feeling possible. Show working on paper. Then use the [placement answer key](placement-key.html).

## Level 1
1. 8 + 7
2. 13 − 6
3. 46 + 30
4. What is 10 less than 300?

## Level 2
5. Write 4,036 as thousands, hundreds, tens and ones.
6. 285 + 347
7. 1,000 − 264
8. Rocket A is 63 m tall. Rocket B is 18 m shorter. How tall is Rocket B? How tall are they together if stacked?

## Level 3
9. 7 × 8
10. 54 ÷ 6
11. How many in 4 rows of 9?
12. Three bags hold 24 rocks shared equally. How many rocks are in 5 such bags?

## Level 6
21. Draw a rectangle and shade 3/4 of it.
22. Which is larger, 2/3 or 3/5? Explain.
23. 1/4 + 2/4

## Level 7
25. Find the perimeter of a rectangle 7 cm by 4 cm.
26. Find its area.
27. How many centimetres are in 3 metres?
28. How many 1 cm cubes fill a box 3 cm by 4 cm by 5 cm?

## Level 8
29. What do the three angles of a triangle add up to?
30. How many degrees are in a right angle?
31. A circle has radius 6 cm. What is its diameter?
32. How many lines of symmetry does a square have?

## Problem-solving readiness (everyone tries these)
33. How many squares of any size are there in a 3 × 3 grid?
34. I am a two-digit number. My digits add up to 9, and I divide exactly by 4. What could I be? Find every answer.
35. Four friends each shake hands once with each other. How many handshakes?
