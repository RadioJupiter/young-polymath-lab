Real mathematics, learned through the real universe. Every unit is built around space and physics: the distances to the planets, the speed of light, orbits, telescopes and experiments you can do at home. The mathematics is serious: strong foundations, hard problems and the joy of cracking them.

## How it works

- **The language of instruction is English.** Spanish and Latin are short daily subjects.
- **You move up by mastery, not by birthday.** When you have mastered a level, you go on. When something is not yet solid, you stay with it until it is.
- **Every day has a fixed order:** mental math, a core lesson, challenge problems, a math circle problem, Spanish, Latin, and notebook time.
- **Hard problems are the point.** Being stuck is normal and useful. Try for a while, try a different way, draw a picture, then ask for a hint.
- **Every number about space here is real**, taken from NASA and other authoritative sources and rounded honestly. Nothing is made up to look exciting; it doesn't need to be. Classroom design numbers (for example, an invented habitat box) are labeled as invented and are not used as measurements of Mars.
