Mental math warm-up

10 minutes · say the answer, then explain how you got it

Learn

Ten 100s make 1,000, as in Level 1. Ten 1,000s make 10,000. Adding 1,000 changes the thousands place: 4,036 + 1,000 = 5,036.

Remember

Ten thousands make 10,000. Adding or subtracting 1,000 changes the thousands digit (and may make a new ten-thousand).

Watch me

What is 4,036 + 1,000?

  1. 4,036 has 4 thousands. One more thousand makes 5 thousands.
  2. The other places stay: 5,036.

Together

What is 4,036 − 1,000?

Hint 1

4 thousands minus 1 thousand is 3 thousands.

Hint 2

The other places stay: 3,036.

Check

Add or subtract 1,000, or name 10,000.

  1. 3,500 + 1,000
  2. 10,000 − 1,000
  3. How many thousands make 10,000?

Practice

  1. 8,036 + 1,000
  2. 4,036 − 30
  3. 2,000 + 2,000

Core lesson: place value to 10,000, and a rocket’s height

45 minutes · concrete → pictorial → abstract

Learn

Level 1 used ones, tens, and hundreds, up to 1,000. Today adds thousands. Ten hundreds make 1,000. Ten thousands make 10,000.

1 0001 0001 0001 0001 0001 0001 0001 0001 0001 000

The number 4,036 is 4 thousands, 0 hundreds, 3 tens, and 6 ones. A 0 still holds a place: there are no hundreds, so a 0 is written there.

1 0001 0001 0001 000101010111111

We write 4,036 with a comma in the American way used on this site: 4 thousands, then 036.

NASA, in “What Was the Saturn V?”, says the Saturn V rocket was 111 meters (363 feet) tall. This level uses 111 m from that page, and does not invent a finer official height.

An invented classroom rocket, labeled invented, is 63 m tall. 63 is 6 tens and 3 ones, not an official NASA height. Tomorrow a bar model will compare heights; today the job is to read and write the numbers, including 4,036 and 10,000, and to keep the NASA height and the invented height labeled as what they are.

The Saturn V height used in this level is from NASA, “What Was the Saturn V?”: the Saturn V rocket was 111 meters (363 feet) tall. https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/for-kids-and-students/what-was-the-saturn-v/ Classroom rocket heights (for example 63 m, and 18 m shorter) and classroom shadow lengths are invented and labeled invented. They are not official NASA measurements. This level does not average 111 m with any other figure.

Remember

4,036 is 4 thousands, 0 hundreds, 3 tens, 6 ones. Ten thousands make 10,000. Saturn V is 111 m (363 feet) on the NASA student page cited. A 63 m classroom rocket is invented and labeled invented.

Watch me

Write 4,036 as thousands, hundreds, tens, and ones, and say why the 0 is there.

  1. 4 thousands, 0 hundreds, 3 tens, 6 ones.
  2. The 0 holds the hundreds place so the 4 stays thousands. Without it, 436 would be a different number.

Together

What height does this lesson use for the Saturn V, and what must you say about a 63 m classroom rocket?

Hint 1

111 meters (363 feet), from the NASA page named in the lesson.

Hint 2

63 m is invented and labeled invented, not an official NASA height.

Check

These read numbers to 10,000, or keep the two heights labeled.

  1. What number is 7 thousands, 2 hundreds, 0 tens, and 5 ones?
  2. What is 10,000 in thousands?

Practice

  1. Write 4,036 and 10,000 in a place-value chart (Th, H, T, O).
  2. A student writes Saturn V as 63 m because “that was the rocket in the problem.” What was mixed?

Challenge problems

25 minutes · try every one; being stuck for a while is part of the work

Learn

10 more than 4,036 is 4,046. 10 less is 4,026. 100 more is 4,136. 1,000 more is 5,036. The place you change must match the 10, 100, or 1,000.

Remember

Change the place that matches what you add or subtract. A 0 in a place still holds that place.

Watch me

What is 100 more than 4,036, and what is 10 less than 4,036?

  1. 4,136.
  2. 4,026.

Together

What is 10,000 − 1? Use places, not a guess.

Hint 1

10,000 is 10 thousands. Taking one one means regrouping.

Hint 2

9,999: 9 thousands, 9 hundreds, 9 tens, 9 ones.

Check

These stretch places up to 10,000.

  1. Which is greater, 4,036 or 4,306? Why?
  2. What is 4,000 + 36?

Practice

  1. Write 10 times 100, and 10 times 1,000.
  2. The Saturn V is 111 m on the NASA page. An invented rocket is 63 m. How much taller is 111 than 63? Label both figures.

Math circle: why a 0 holds a place, up to 10,000

20 minutes · one rich problem, solved and then explained

Learn

In 4,036 the 0 is not “nothing.” It holds the hundreds so that 4 can mean thousands. 4,036 and 436 and 4,360 are three different numbers.

Remember

A zero can hold a place. The place of a digit is what gives it its value.

Watch me

Why is 4,036 not the same as 436, and not the same as 4,360?

  1. 4,036 is 4 thousands and 36; 436 is 4 hundreds and 36, with no thousands.
  2. 4,360 is 4 thousands, 3 hundreds, and 6 tens, with 0 ones — the 3 is in a different place than in 4,036.

Together

A student says 10,000 is “a 1 and four 0s, so it is the same as 1,000 because both have zeros.” What is wrong?

Hint 1

The number of zeros, and the place of the 1, are different.

Hint 2

10,000 is ten thousands; 1,000 is ten hundreds, or one thousand.

Check

These defend place value with a short argument.

  1. Why must you write a 0 in 4,036?
  2. How many hundreds are in 10,000?

Practice

  1. Write a short argument that 4,036 + 1,000 is 5,036, not 4,136.
  2. Why does this lesson not replace 111 m with 63 m for the Saturn V?

Spanish · Alto, bajo, más, and menos

15 minutes · Method: hear and point. Instruction in English.

Learn

Today names alto, bajo, más, menos, and ¿cuánto?. Level 1 greetings and uno–diez may be reused, not retaught as a first look.

Word How to say it Meaning
alto AHL-toh tall / high
bajo BAH-hoh short / low
más mahs more
menos MEH-nohs less
¿cuánto? KWAHN-toh how much?

The guide points at a taller drawing and says es alto, then a shorter one and es bajo, then más alto / menos alto. The student points, then may speak. Do not retell the Saturn V fact in Spanish.

Instructions are in English. Hear clips use es-MX. Greetings and numbers 1–10 from Level 1 may be reused, not retaught as a first look. If a student began at Level 2, the guide may first use Level 1 Days 1–2 Spanish for hola and uno–diez, then continue here.

Remember

Alto: tall or high. Bajo: short or low. Más: more. Menos: less. ¿Cuánto?: how much? Instruction stays in English.

Watch me

The guide points at the taller of two invented rockets and says es más alto. What should the student do, and what does the phrase mean?

  1. Point at the taller rocket.
  2. It is taller (more tall).

Together

What does menos mean, and what does bajo mean?

Hint 1

Menos: less.

Hint 2

Bajo: short / low.

Check

These check the new words.

  1. Give the English of alto, más, and ¿cuánto?.
  2. Hear bajo and menos. Give the English of each.

Exercises

  1. Say the Spanish for “more” and for “tall.”
  2. Why does this block not give the Saturn V height in Spanish?

Latin · Alta, and umbra

15 minutes · Method: a short original caption. Original sentences only.

Learn

Today names alta (tall / high, in the feminine form used with a word like umbra or a feminine noun in a caption) and umbra (a shadow), still with original captions.

Word How to say it Meaning
alta AHL-tah tall / high (feminine form, as in a caption)
umbra OOM-brah a shadow
est est is

The Hear-word button uses an Italian voice as an approximation of Latin, not the speech of ancient Rome.

Original captions:

  • Umbra est. — It is a shadow.
  • Umbra alta est. — The shadow is tall (a long / high shadow in the picture).

Do not invent a full declension table. The job is these captions. Level 1’s sōl, lūna, diēs, and nox may be reused, not retaught as first looks.

All Latin here is original. Explanations are in English. The Hear-word button uses an Italian voice as an approximation of Latin, not the speech of ancient Rome. Vowels are pure; c like k; v like English w.

Remember

Umbra: a shadow. Alta: tall / high, in the form used in today’s captions. Umbra est and Umbra alta est are original captions.

Watch me

Point at a long shadow in a drawing and say a Latin caption, then the English.

  1. Umbra alta est. (Or Umbra est, if only naming it.)
  2. The shadow is tall / it is a tall shadow. Or: it is a shadow.

Together

What does umbra mean?

Hint 1

A shadow.

Check

These check umbra and alta.

  1. Give the English of Umbra est.
  2. What does alta mean in today’s captions?

Exercises

  1. Hear umbra and alta. Give the English of Umbra alta est.
  2. Write one original caption for a shadow drawing.

Notebook: 4,036 in places, and two rocket heights

60 minutes · draw first, then label, then write · How the notebook works

Learn

Draw first: a place-value chart for 4,036, and two rockets labeled Saturn V 111 m (NASA) and classroom rocket 63 m (invented). Then write from stems.

Word bank: thousands, hundreds, tens, ones, meters, invented, NASA, Saturn V.

Stems: 1. 4,036 has a 0 in the hundreds place because … 2. The classroom rocket is 63 m, but … 3. The Saturn V is 111 m, so …

End with both heights written with their labels, and one “what next” question.

Remember

Draw the chart and the two labeled rockets first. Keep NASA and invented heights distinct in the sentences.

Watch me

Finish: “4,036 has a 0 in the hundreds place because …”

  1. A model: “… because the 0 holds that place so the 4 stays thousands.” Other complete finishes accepted.

Together

Finish: “The classroom rocket is 63 m, but …”

Hint 1

It is not the Saturn V height.

Hint 2

A model: “… but that height is invented, not the NASA figure.”

Check

These check the labels and the places.

  1. What two heights must appear, and with what labels?
  2. What do you draw first?

Practice

  1. Finish all three stems in writing.
  2. Write one “what next” question about comparing the two heights.
