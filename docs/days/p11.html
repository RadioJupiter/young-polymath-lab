Mental math warm-up

10 minutes · say the answer, then explain how you got it

Learn

Counting in equal steps is the start of multiplication. 8, 16, 24, 32 are four 8s. A week has 7 days, so two weeks have 14 days: 7 + 7.

Remember

Equal jumps name equal groups. Two 7s are 14. A week is 7 days (calendar), not a NASA period.

Watch me

How many days are in two weeks, and how do you know?

  1. 7 + 7 = 14, or two 7s.

Together

Count four 8s: 8, 16, 24, … What is the fourth number, and how many 8s is that?

Hint 1

The next jump is 24 + 8 = 32.

Hint 2

Four 8s.

Check

Equal jumps and sevens.

  1. Two weeks in days
  2. Four 8s
  3. 3 + 3 + 3

Practice

  1. Five 7s (days in five weeks)
  2. 2 + 2 + 2 + 2
  3. 10 + 10 + 10

Core lesson: equal groups, and 7 × 8

45 minutes · concrete → pictorial → abstract

Learn

Multiplication is a short way to write equal groups. 7 × 8 means 7 groups of 8, or 8 + 8 + 8 + 8 + 8 + 8 + 8. It is also 8 groups of 7. The product is the same: 56.

Concrete: seven small piles of 8 counters (invented classroom piles, labeled invented). Count in 8s: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56.

Pictorial: an array. An array is equal rows with the same number in each row. 7 rows of 8:

You may also turn the array: 8 rows of 7 is the same 56 marks. So 7 × 8 = 8 × 7 = 56. That is the commutative fact: the groups can be swapped; the product stays the same. The word is used here for this one idea, not as a long new theory.

Abstract: 7 × 8 = 56, and 8 × 7 = 56. A week has 7 days. Eight weeks have 8 × 7 = 56 days. That 56 is a calendar count of days, not a NASA month. The NASA month in this level comes later in the week.

The length of the month used in this level is from the NASA Moon fact sheet: the Moon’s synodic period is 29.53 days, taught here as about 29½ days. https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html This level does not invent a finer official length, and does not begin the sidereal month or Moon phases as fractions (those belong later). Classroom bag, rock, and array counts are invented and labeled invented. They are not official NASA counts. A week of 7 days is a calendar fact used here, not a NASA orbital period.

Remember

7 × 8 means 7 groups of 8, or 8 groups of 7, and equals 56. An array shows equal rows. 7 × 8 = 8 × 7. Classroom arrays are invented and labeled invented. Eight weeks are 56 days, a calendar count, not a NASA month.

Watch me

Show 7 × 8 as equal groups or as an array, and give the product.

  1. 7 groups of 8, or 7 rows of 8 (or 8 rows of 7).
  2. The product is 56.

Together

Why is 7 × 8 the same as 8 × 7?

Hint 1

The same array can be seen as 7 rows of 8 or 8 rows of 7.

Hint 2

The number of marks does not change when you turn the array.

Check

These use equal groups or 7 × 8.

  1. Find 7 × 8 and 8 × 7.
  2. How many days are in eight weeks?

Practice

  1. Draw or describe an array for 4 × 5, and give the product.
  2. A student writes 7 × 8 = 15 because 7 + 8 = 15. What was mixed?

Challenge problems

25 minutes · try every one; being stuck for a while is part of the work

Learn

If 7 × 8 = 56, then 7 × 9 is one more 7: 56 + 7 = 63. Or one more 8 than 6 × 8, if you know that fact. Near facts use a fact you already have.

Remember

A nearby product can be found from a known product by adding or taking one equal group.

Watch me

Using 7 × 8 = 56, what is 7 × 9?

  1. One more 7: 56 + 7 = 63.

Together

What is 6 × 8, using 7 × 8 if it helps?

Hint 1

6 eights is one 8 less than 7 eights.

Hint 2

56 − 8 = 48.

Check

These stretch a known product.

  1. Find 7 × 10 (ten 7s, or 7 × 8 plus two more 7s).
  2. Find 8 × 8, using 7 × 8 if it helps.

Practice

  1. Why is 7 × 8 not 78?
  2. How many days are in 3 weeks?

Math circle: why turning an array does not change the product

20 minutes · one rich problem, solved and then explained

Learn

An array of 7 rows of 8 has the same marks as 8 rows of 7. The picture is turned; the marks are not added or taken away. That is why 7 × 8 = 8 × 7.

Remember

The product does not change when the groups are swapped. That is one array, seen two ways.

Watch me

Why is 7 × 8 equal to 8 × 7, without just saying “that is the rule”?

  1. The same 56 marks can be counted as 7 rows of 8 or as 8 rows of 7.
  2. Turning the array does not add or remove marks.

Together

A student says 7 × 8 and 8 × 7 must be different because “the numbers are in a different order.” What would you show?

Hint 1

One array, two views.

Hint 2

Count both ways: both are 56.

Check

These defend the array.

  1. What stays the same when you turn a 7-by-8 array?
  2. What changes in the way you say the groups?

Practice

  1. Write a short argument that 7 × 8 = 56, with equal groups or an array.
  2. Why is “eight weeks are 56 days” not a NASA month?

Spanish · Semana, día, and grupos

15 minutes · Method: hear and point. Instruction in English.

Learn

Today names semana, día, siete, and grupos. Numbers already taught may be reused, not retaught as a first look.

Word How to say it Meaning
semana seh-MAH-nah week
día DEE-ah day
siete SYEH-teh seven
grupos GROO-pohs groups

The guide points at a seven-day calendar strip (invented classroom strip, labeled invented) and says una semana tiene siete días. The student points, then may speak. Do not retell the NASA month in Spanish.

Instructions are in English. Hear clips use es-MX. Words from Levels 1 and 2 may be reused, not retaught as a first look. If a student began at Level 3, the guide may first use earlier foundation Spanish, then continue here.

Remember

Semana: week. Día: day. Siete: seven. Grupos: groups. Una semana tiene siete días. Instruction stays in English.

Watch me

The guide says una semana tiene siete días. What should the student do, and what does the sentence mean?

  1. Point at the seven-day strip, or show seven.
  2. A week has seven days.

Together

What does grupos mean, and what does día mean?

Hint 1

Grupos: groups.

Hint 2

Día: day.

Check

These check the new words.

  1. Give the English of semana, siete, and día.
  2. Hear grupos. Give the English, and say the sentence about a week.

Exercises

  1. Say the Spanish for “week” and for “seven.”
  2. Why does this block not give 29½ days in Spanish?

Notebook: 7 × 8 as equal groups, and eight weeks

60 minutes · draw first, then label, then write · How the notebook works

Learn

Draw first: 7 groups of 8, or a 7-by-8 array, labeled invented marks, and a small note that eight weeks are 56 days (calendar), not a NASA month. Then write from stems.

Word bank: groups, array, product, fifty-six, weeks, calendar, invented.

Stems: 1. 7 × 8 is 56 because … 2. I could have added 7 + 8 and written 15, but … 3. Eight weeks are 56 days, so …

End with 7 × 8 = 56 and 8 × 7 = 56, and one “what next” question.

Remember

Draw the groups or array first. The but-stem should refuse adding 7 and 8 as if that were the product.

Watch me

Finish: “7 × 8 is 56 because …”

  1. A model: “… because it is seven 8s, or 7 rows of 8.” Other complete finishes accepted.

Together

Finish the but-stem about 7 + 8 = 15.

Hint 1

15 is the sum, not seven 8s.

Check

These check the product in writing.

  1. What two multiplications should appear at the end?
  2. What must you not call the 56 days of eight weeks?

Practice

  1. Finish all three stems in writing.
  2. Write one “what next” question about an array with a different number of rows.
