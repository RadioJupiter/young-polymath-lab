Mental math warm-up: hundredths, and a tenth of a quantity

10 minutes · say the answer, then explain how you got it

Learn

Percent means per hundred, so it uses the hundredths you already know from Level 9. A useful mental step is to find 10% first, because 10% is one tenth.

Remember

0.25 = 25/100 = 1/4. 10% of a quantity is the quantity ÷ 10. 50% is half. 25% is a quarter.

Watch me

What is 10% of 80, and what is 50% of 80?

  1. 10% is one tenth, so 80 ÷ 10 = 8.
  2. 50% is half, so 80 ÷ 2 = 40.
  3. We will name these as percents in the core; the jumps are already familiar.

Together

Write 0.25 as hundredths, and as a fraction of a whole in lowest terms.

Hint 1: Hundredths

0.25 is 25 hundredths, or 25/100.

Hint 2: Lowest terms

25/100 = 1/4.

Check

These use only hundredths and tenths of a quantity.

  1. What is 10% of 40?
  2. Write 1/2 as hundredths.

Practice

  1. What is 25% of 80, if 25% is a quarter?
  2. Write 0.1 as hundredths.

Core lesson: percent as per hundred

35–40 minutes · Method: pictorial (a percent bar and a hundred-square), then percent as a hundredth and as a part of a named whole

Learn

Percent means per hundred. We write 25% for 25 per 100. That is the same amount as the fraction 25/100, which is 1/4, and the same amount as the decimal 0.25 you already used in Level 9. The pictured bar is one whole, split into ten equal tenths (each tenth is 10%). Two and a half tenths are shaded, so 25% of the bar is shaded.

A hundred-square from Level 9 can be read as percents too: each small square is 1%. 25 shaded squares are 25%.

Percent of a quantity. To find 25% of 80, we are finding 25/100 of 80, or a quarter of 80, which is 20. Useful mental paths, once they are taught:

  • 100% of a quantity is the whole of that quantity.
  • 50% is half of it.
  • 25% is a quarter of it.
  • 10% is a tenth of it (divide by 10). 5% is half of 10%.

So 10% of 80 is 8, and 5% of 80 is 4. We can also find 20% as two lots of 10%, and 30% as three lots of 10%.

Finding a percent. If 40 beads in an invented bag of 200 beads are red, the red part is 40/200. 40/200 = 20/100 = 20%. We divide the numerator and the denominator by the same number until the denominator is 100, or we first write an equivalent fraction over 100. The bag is invented, so 20% stays a percent of that invented whole.

The whole must be named. 25% of 80 is 20, but 25% of 40 is 10. A percent without a named whole is not yet a quantity. We will not invent a “NASA percent” (for example a homemade “75% of Mars is…”) and call it sourced.

Remember

Percent means per hundred. 25% = 25/100 = 1/4 = 0.25. 50% is half, 25% is a quarter, 10% is a tenth, 5% is half of 10%. A percent of a quantity needs a named whole. Invented collections stay labeled. We will not invent a NASA percent.

Watch me

Find 25% of 40, and write 25% as a decimal and as a fraction in lowest terms.

  1. 25% is a quarter, so 25% of 40 is 40 ÷ 4 = 10.
  2. 25% = 0.25 = 25/100 = 1/4.
  3. The 40 can be an invented classroom collection, labeled invented.

Together

An invented box holds 50 tiles, and 10 of them are blue. What percent are blue?

Hint 1: The fraction

10 out of 50 is 10/50.

Hint 2: Per hundred

10/50 = 20/100 = 20%. The box is invented.

Check

These use only today’s percents.

  1. Write 50% as a fraction in lowest terms, and find 50% of 90.
  2. What is 10% of 250, and what is 5% of 250?

Practice

  1. Find 30% of 80 (find 10% first).
  2. An invented pack has 200 cards, and 40 are marked. What percent are marked?
  3. Why is “25% is 25” not a complete answer to “what is 25% of 40?”
  4. Write 0.4 as a percent. (0.4 is 40 hundredths.)
  5. Why will this course not write “75% of the Moon is rock, as a NASA fact from this lesson”?

Challenge problems: mixed percents, and a named whole

20 minutes · after the core, or later if the core took the full sitting

Learn

These items mix percent of a quantity and writing a part as a percent. The whole must be named. Invented collections stay labeled. We will not invent a NASA percent.

Remember

Same percent method as the core. Name the whole. Labels stay honest.

Watch me

Find 15% of 80 (find 10% and 5%), and write 15% as a decimal.

  1. 10% of 80 is 8; 5% of 80 is 4; 15% is 8 + 4 = 12.
  2. 15% = 15/100 = 0.15.
  3. The 80 can be invented, labeled invented.

Together

An invented tray holds 25 apples, and 5 are green. What percent are green, and what percent are not green?

Hint 1: Green

5/25 = 20/100 = 20%.

Hint 2: The rest

The rest is 20 apples, or 100% − 20% = 80%. The tray is invented.

Check

These stay with percents and named wholes.

  1. Find 40% of 50 (find 10% first).
  2. Write 3/4 as a percent.

Practice

  1. An invented class has 24 seats, and 18 are filled. What percent are filled? (18/24 first.)
  2. Why is 10% of 30 not 10?
  3. Find 5% of 60.
  4. A friend says 200% “cannot exist, because a bar only goes to 100.” What would 200% of 15 be, if 200% means two wholes of the named quantity? (A first look only.)
  5. This wrap is not a second score.

Math circle: a percent needs a named whole

20 minutes · a short reason, then a check

Learn

25% is 25 per 100, which is a part of whatever whole we have named. 25% of 80 and 25% of 40 are different quantities, both honestly 25%. A percent is not a free-standing pile of 25 objects. That is the same honesty habit as labeling a NASA figure: the number has a job, and the job needs a label.

Remember

Percent means per hundred. 25% = 1/4 = 0.25. The whole must be named. We will not invent a NASA percent.

Watch me

Why is 25% of 80 equal to 20, while 25% of 40 is only 10, if both are “25%”?

  1. 25% means 25 per 100 of the named whole.
  2. A quarter of 80 is 20; a quarter of 40 is 10.
  3. The percent is the same comparison; the wholes are different, so the amounts are different.

Together

Finish: I will write 20% of 200 as 40 because ________, but I will not write “20%” as if it were the number 20, so ________.

Hint 1: The because

…20% is 20 per 100, and 20/100 of 200 is 40…

Hint 2: The so

…I will name the whole, and I will not treat the 20 as a pile of 20 objects.

Check

These close the reason.

  1. Write 10% as a fraction and as a decimal.
  2. What is 100% of 36?

Practice

  1. Give one reason 50% of 18 is 9, not 50.
  2. Why does a homemade “75% of the Moon is rock, from NASA” fail today’s honesty habit?
  3. How does 25% sit beside 0.25 and 1/4?
  4. If two invented bags are both “30% red,” do they have the same number of red items? What else do you need to know?
  5. This argument is not a badge.

Spanish · cien, todo, and la mitad

15 minutes · Instruction in English; Hear for Spanish words · present tense only

Learn

Word How to say it Meaning
cien syehn a hundred
todo TOH-doh all / everything (masculine singular)
la mitad lah mee-TAHD the half
hay cien ai syehn there are a hundred

Today’s words name a hundred, all, and the half. Instruction stays in English. Percent calculations stay in the English mathematics: we will not practise “25% of 80” in Spanish.

Original sentences: Hay cien. Es todo. Es la mitad. No es todo.

For the guide: show a full set of 100 small squares if you have a hundred-square, then cover half of a simple drawing. This is present-tense description, not a percent lesson in Spanish.

Remember

cien; todo; la mitad; hay cien. Instruction in English. Hear for Spanish words. No percent arithmetic in the Spanish.

Watch me

Say: “There are a hundred.”

  1. Hay cien.
  2. Hay introduces them. Cien is a hundred.
  3. This is a present-tense pattern, not a percent calculation.

Together

How do you say “the half,” and “It is all”?

Hint 1: The half

la mitad

Hint 2: The sentence

Es todo.

Check

These use only today’s phrases.

  1. Write: “It is the half.”
  2. Write: “It is not all.”

Exercises

  1. Correct: *El mitad, if someone used the wrong article.
  2. Hear cien, todo, and mitad, then use each.
  3. Why does the Spanish not say 25% or 0.25?
  4. A pointing retell: a hundred, all, the half.
  5. Copy one model sentence into the notebook.

Latin · centum and dīmidium

15 minutes · Method: picture-based Latin; original sentences. Instruction in English.

Learn

Word How to say it Meaning
centum KEN-tum a hundred (indeclinable)
Centum adsunt KEN-tum AHD-sunt A hundred are here
dīmidium dee-MID-ee-um a half (nominative, neuter)
Dīmidium est dee-MID-ee-um est It is a half

The Hear-word button uses an Italian voice as an approximation of Latin, not the speech of ancient Rome.

Original sentences, not from a textbook: Centum adsunt. Dīmidium est. Centum nōn adsunt. Dīmidium nōn est.

centum does not change its ending. dīmidium is neuter. Yesterday’s tōtum can be pointed to again (the whole), but it is not required in today’s check. Percent signs and hundredths arithmetic stay in the English mathematics.

For the guide: the student reads as much as possible. Point to a full hundred-square, then to half of a simple drawing. Italian-voice Hear, labeled as an approximation.

Remember

centum; Centum adsunt; dīmidium; Dīmidium est. centum does not change its ending. No percent arithmetic in the Latin. Italian-voice Hear, labeled as an approximation.

Watch me

Read: Centum adsunt. Why adsunt, not *adest?

  1. A hundred things are more than one.
  2. adsunt is the form for more than one.
  3. adest would be used for one thing only.

Together

Say: “It is a half.”

Hint 1: The noun

dīmidium, neuter.

Hint 2: The sentence

Dīmidium est.

Check

These use only today’s patterns.

  1. Write: “A hundred are not here.”
  2. Write: “It is not a half.”

Exercises

  1. Correct: *centum adest if it appears (adsunt for more than one).
  2. Hear centum and dīmidium, then use each in a sentence.
  3. Draw a hundred marks, or a hundred-square, and a half, labeled in Latin.
  4. Why are 25% and 0.25 left in the English mathematics?
  5. Copy one model sentence.

Notebook: a percent bar, and a because-sentence

60 minutes · draw first, then label, then write · How the notebook works · Review sheet

Learn

Draw a percent bar (or a hundred-square) showing 25% of a named whole, and write 25% = 25/100 = 1/4 = 0.25. Then complete a because / but / so stem about naming the whole, or about not inventing a NASA percent.

Remember

Draw and label first. Complete stems. Name the whole. This page is a record, not a score or a badge.

Watch me

A model sentence: 25% of 80 is 20 because 25% is a quarter, but 25% of 40 is only 10, so I will name the whole and I will not write “25%” as if it were the number 25.

  1. The because uses percent of a named whole.
  2. The but changes the whole and the amount.
  3. The so states the habit.

Together

Finish: I can find 20% of an invented bag of 200 because ________, but I will not write “75% of the Moon is rock, from NASA,” so ________.

Hint 1: The because

…20% is 20 per 100, and 20/100 of 200 is 40, and the bag is labeled invented…

Hint 2: The so

…I will not give an invented percent a NASA label this lesson did not source.

Check

The page should show the bar and complete stems.

  1. Draw a 25% bar or hundred-square, with the equalities 25% = 25/100 = 1/4 = 0.25, and name a whole.
  2. Write a three-stem explanation (because / but / so) about naming the whole, or about invented percents.

Practice

  1. Tick the word-bank words you used: percent, hundred, whole, quarter, tenth, invented, named.
  2. Add one 10%-then-30% calculation, if it helps the page.
  3. The page is a record, not a score.
  4. File the page. It is not a reward.
  5. A note that a percent needs a named whole.
