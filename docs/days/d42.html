Mental math warm-up: comparing and skip-counting

10 minutes · say the answer, then explain how you got it

Learn

A graph is easier to read when you can skip-count the scale and compare two numbers quickly.

Remember

Skip-count by twos. The larger of two counts is the one farther along the number line.

Watch me

Skip-count by twos from 0 to 12. What are the numbers?

  1. 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12.

Together

Which is greater, 10 or 6, and by how much?

Hint 1: Compare

10 is greater than 6.

Hint 2: The difference

10 − 6 = 4.

Check

These are short compares and skip-counts.

  1. Skip-count by twos from 0 to 8.
  2. 8 compared with 2: how much greater is 8?

Practice

  1. 4 + 4 + 4.
  2. Which is smaller, 2 or 6?

Core lesson: bar graphs and pictographs

40 minutes · Method: the same invented table, first as bars on an even scale, then as pictures with a key

Learn

A bar graph uses bars of different lengths to show the same counts. The scale is the even set of numbers along the side. On the graph above, the scale goes from 0 to 12 in steps of 2. Every bar starts at 0, and its height is read against that scale. Watch 4 reaches 10; Watch 3 reaches 2.

The scale must be even: the same step all the way up. If the steps suddenly jumped, a taller bar could no longer be trusted as a larger count.

A pictograph uses a picture, repeated, to show the same counts. It needs a key, which tells you what one picture stands for. In the pictograph above, one star stands for 2 meteors. Watch 2 has four stars, and 4 × 2 = 8, which matches the table from Day 41. Watch 3 has one star, which is 2 meteors.

Without the key, a reader cannot tell whether one star means 1 meteor, 2 meteors, or 10.

These graphs show the same invented classroom meteor watch as Day 41. They do not add a new official rate.

Remember

A bar graph needs an even scale, and every bar is read from 0 against that scale. A pictograph needs a key that says what one picture stands for. The same invented counts can be shown both ways. Invented counts stay labeled invented.

Watch me

On the bar graph, which watch is tallest, and what count does its height show?

  1. Watch 4 is the tallest bar.
  2. It reaches 10 on the scale, so the count is 10.

Together

On the pictograph, Watch 5 has three stars, and the key says one star = 2 meteors. How many meteors is that?

Hint 1: The key

Each star stands for 2 meteors.

Hint 2: The product

3 × 2 = 6, which matches the table.

Check

These use the graphs above, or a new labeled invented set.

  1. How much greater is Watch 2 than Watch 1 on the bar graph?
  2. A new pictograph uses the key “one square = 5.” A row shows 3 squares. What count does that row show?

Practice

  1. Why would a scale that went 0, 2, 4, 10, 12 be a problem on a bar graph?
  2. Why is a pictograph without a key not ready to be read?

Challenge problems: a key, a half-picture, and a missing bar

25 minutes · harder work with the same graph skills

Learn

Sometimes a pictograph uses a half-picture when the key is even, and sometimes a bar is missing and must be recovered from a total. The counts below are invented and labeled.

Remember

A key is used for every picture, including a half-picture when the key is even. A missing bar can be found from a total if the other bars are known.

Watch me

A labeled invented pictograph has the key “one circle = 2.” A row shows 3½ circles. What count does that row show?

  1. Three circles stand for 3 × 2 = 6.
  2. A half-circle stands for 1, so the row shows 7.

Together

A labeled invented bar graph of four watches has a total of 28. The first three bars are 5, 8, and 6. What must the fourth bar be?

Hint 1: The known part

5 + 8 + 6 = 19.

Hint 2: The missing bar

28 − 19 = 9.

Check

These stay with bars, pictographs, and labeled invented counts.

  1. A key says “one square = 4.” A row must show 10. How many squares should be drawn, including any half?
  2. Two bars on an even scale show 3 and 9. How many times as tall is the second bar as the first?

Practice

  1. Why can a pictograph with the key “one star = 2” not show a count of 7 with whole stars only?

Math circle: why the scale must be even, and why a key is required

20 minutes · a reason about reading a graph honestly

Learn

A graph is a picture of numbers. If the scale jumps, or the key is missing, the picture can no longer be trusted as a fair comparison.

Remember

An even scale keeps a taller bar as a larger count. A key tells a reader what one picture stands for. Without either, the graph is not ready to be read.

Watch me

A bar graph’s scale is marked 0, 2, 4, then a wide gap, then 20. A bar is drawn halfway up that wide gap. Why can a reader not be sure of the count?

  1. The steps are not even, so halfway through the gap is not a number the scale has defined.
  2. The bar’s height no longer matches a steady step.
  3. The graph cannot be read honestly until the scale is even.

Together

A pictograph is printed with four stars in one row and no key. One person says the count is 4, and another says it is 8. Who is right?

Hint 1: The missing key

Neither person can be sure, because the key was not given.

Hint 2: Both readings

One star might mean 1, or 2, or something else.

Check

These check the reason.

  1. Give one reason every bar should start at 0 on the scale, not at 2.
  2. Why does changing a key from “one star = 2” to “one star = 5,” without changing the pictures, change the counts?

Practice

  1. A friend draws a bar graph with a lovely even scale, but forgets to label what the scale counts. What is still missing?

Spanish · el gráfico, la barra, la clave, and alta

15 minutes · Instruction in English; Hear for Spanish words · present tense only

Learn

Word How to say it Meaning
el gráfico el GRAH-fee-koh the graph
la barra lah BAH-rrah the bar (on a bar graph)
la clave lah KLAH-beh the key (what one picture stands for)
alta AHL-tah tall (feminine)

Today’s words name a graph, a bar, a key, and “tall.” Instruction stays in English. Scales, products from a key, and invented counts stay in the English mathematics.

Original sentences: Hay un gráfico. Hay una barra. La barra es alta. Veo la clave.

For the guide: point to the bar graph, to the tallest bar, then to the pictograph key, as you say the words. This is present-tense description, not a scale lesson in Spanish.

Remember

el gráfico; la barra; la clave; alta. Instruction in English. Hear for Spanish words. No scale arithmetic in the Spanish.

Watch me

Say: “There is a graph.”

  1. Hay un gráfico.
  2. Hay introduces it. Un gráfico is the graph.

Together

How do you say “The bar is tall”?

Hint 1: The bar

la barra

Hint 2: The sentence

La barra es alta.

Check

These use only today’s phrases.

  1. Write: “There is a bar.”
  2. Write: “I see the key.”

Exercises

  1. Correct: *El clave, if someone used the wrong article.
  2. Hear gráfico, barra, clave, and alta, then use each.
  3. Why does the Spanish not multiply 3 × 2 for a pictograph key?
  4. A pointing retell: a graph, a bar, tall, a key.
  5. Copy one model sentence into the notebook.

Latin · columna and clāvis

15 minutes · Method: picture-based Latin; original sentences. Instruction in English.

Learn

Word How to say it Meaning
columna kol-LUM-nah a column, a bar (nominative, feminine)
Columna alta est kol-LUM-nah AHL-tah est The column is tall
clāvis KLAH-wis a key (nominative, feminine)
Clāvis adest KLAH-wis AH-dest A key is here

The Hear-word button uses an Italian voice as an approximation of Latin, not the speech of ancient Rome.

Original sentences, not from a textbook: Columna alta est. Columna nōn alta est. Clāvis adest. Clāvis nōn adest.

columna and clāvis are feminine, so alta matches columna. Scale steps and pictograph products stay in the English mathematics.

For the guide: the student reads as much as possible. Point to a tall bar, then to the pictograph key. Italian-voice Hear, labeled as an approximation. clāvis here is the graph’s key, the same Latin noun as a door key, used as a label for the picture.

Remember

columna; Columna alta est; clāvis; Clāvis adest. Instruction in English. Italian-voice Hear, labeled as an approximation. No scale arithmetic in the Latin.

Watch me

Read: “The column is tall.”

  1. Columna alta est.
  2. columna is the column. alta matches because columna is feminine.

Together

How do you say “A key is here”?

Hint 1: The key

clāvis

Hint 2: The sentence

Clāvis adest.

Check

These use only today’s phrases.

  1. Write: “The column is not tall.”
  2. Write: “A key is not here.”

Exercises

  1. Why is *Columna altus est a miss?
  2. Hear columna and clāvis, then use each in a sentence.
  3. Why does the Latin not read a bar against a scale of twos?
  4. A pointing retell: a column, a key.
  5. Copy one model sentence into the notebook.

Notebook: a bar graph and a pictograph, and a because-sentence

about 1 hour · both graphs of the invented watch, then because / but / so

Learn

Draw a bar graph with an even scale and a pictograph with a key for the same invented watch, then write a because / but / so sentence about the key.

Remember

The page shows the same invented counts two ways. The stems explain why a key is required, and why the scale must be even.

Watch me

Start: The pictograph needs a key because ________.

  1. The because restates that a reader has to know what one picture stands for.
  2. A sound because: …one star might mean 1, or 2, or 5, until the key says…

Together

Finish: The pictograph needs a key because ________, but the pictures alone do not name the amount, so ________.

Hint 1: The but

…a row of stars without a key can be read two ways…

Hint 2: The so

…I will write the key, and I will keep the bar-graph scale even.

Check

The page should show both graphs and complete stems.

  1. Draw a bar graph (scale 0 to 12 in steps of 2) and a pictograph (one star = 2) for the invented watch 4, 8, 2, 10, and 6.
  2. Write a three-stem explanation (because / but / so) about the key and the even scale.

Practice

  1. Tick the word-bank words you used: bar, scale, pictograph, key, even.
  2. Label both graphs as the invented classroom watch.
  3. The page is a record, not a score.
  4. File the page. It is not a reward.
