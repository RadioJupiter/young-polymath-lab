Mental math warm-up

10 minutes · say the answer, then explain how you got it

Learn

To add 47 + 8, make ten with the ones: 47 + 3 + 5 = 50 + 5 = 52. To add 47 + 30, add the tens: 7 tens + 3 tens, 7 ones: 77.

Remember

Make ten with the ones, or add whole tens, as in Level 1. Today those strategies help with larger adds.

Watch me

What is 47 + 8, by making ten?

  1. 47 + 3 = 50, and 8 = 3 + 5.
  2. 50 + 5 = 52.

Together

What is 285 + 40?

Hint 1

40 is 4 tens.

Hint 2

8 tens + 4 tens = 12 tens, which is 1 hundred and 2 tens: 325.

Check

Mental addition with making ten or tens.

  1. 47 + 8
  2. 285 + 40
  3. 199 + 1

Practice

  1. 35 + 27 (make ten)
  2. 500 + 300
  3. 47 + 30

Core lesson: adding with regrouping, and a part-whole bar

45 minutes · concrete → pictorial → abstract

Learn

When ones make 10 or more, they make a new ten. That is regrouping (or trading), the same idea as making ten, now written in columns.

285 + 347.

Concrete: 5 ones + 7 ones = 12 ones, which is 1 ten and 2 ones. 8 tens + 4 tens + 1 ten = 13 tens, which is 1 hundred and 3 tens. 2 hundreds + 3 hundreds + 1 hundred = 6 hundreds. The sum is 632.

Pictorial: a part-whole bar. One bar is split into the two parts; a bracket names the whole.

parts285347
whole = ?

Abstract: columns. Line up the places. Add ones first.

    285
  + 347
  -----
    632

Ones: 5 + 7 = 12, write 2, carry 1 ten. Tens: 8 + 4 + 1 = 13, write 3, carry 1 hundred. Hundreds: 2 + 3 + 1 = 6. 632.

Estimate: 300 + 350 = 650, so 632 is reasonable.

An invented story, labeled invented: 285 cards in one box and 347 in another. The total is the whole. This is not a NASA count.

The Saturn V height used in this level is from NASA, “What Was the Saturn V?”: the Saturn V rocket was 111 meters (363 feet) tall. https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/for-kids-and-students/what-was-the-saturn-v/ Classroom rocket heights (for example 63 m, and 18 m shorter) and classroom shadow lengths are invented and labeled invented. They are not official NASA measurements. This level does not average 111 m with any other figure.

Remember

Add ones first. When a column makes 10 or more, write the ones and carry 1 to the next place. A part-whole bar shows two parts and the whole. 285 + 347 = 632. Estimate to check.

Watch me

Show 285 + 347 in columns, and say what was carried.

  1. Ones: 5 + 7 = 12, write 2, carry 1 ten.
  2. Tens: 8 + 4 + 1 = 13, write 3, carry 1 hundred. Hundreds: 2 + 3 + 1 = 6. The sum is 632.

Together

In the part-whole bar for 285 + 347, what are the parts, and what is the whole?

Hint 1

The parts are 285 and 347.

Hint 2

The whole is 632, the sum.

Check

These add with regrouping, or read a part-whole bar.

  1. Find 186 + 157. Show the carries.
  2. Find 400 + 250.

Practice

  1. Find 285 + 347, and check with an estimate.
  2. Invented (labeled): a 63 m classroom rocket and a 45 m classroom rocket, stacked. What is the whole height? Draw or describe a part-whole bar.

Challenge problems

25 minutes · try every one; being stuck for a while is part of the work

Learn

If you know 285 + 347 = 632, then 285 + 348 is one more: 633. A part-whole bar can have more than two parts, as long as the whole is the sum of the parts.

Remember

One more or one less than a known sum is a near fact, not a new method. The whole is all the parts together.

Watch me

Using 285 + 347 = 632, what is 285 + 348?

  1. 348 is 1 more than 347, so the sum is 1 more than 632: 633.

Together

Find 999 + 1, and say what new place appears.

Hint 1

999 + 1 fills the ones, tens, and hundreds.

Hint 2

The sum is 1,000, one thousand.

Check

These stretch regrouping and the whole.

  1. Find 475 + 268.
  2. A whole is 632. One part is 285. What is the other part?

Practice

  1. Find 2,085 + 1,347 (you may add the thousands, then the rest).
  2. Why is 285 + 347 not 5,312?

Math circle: why we carry, and why we estimate

20 minutes · one rich problem, solved and then explained

Learn

Carrying is not a trick. 12 ones are 1 ten and 2 ones, because ten ones make a ten. An estimate (300 + 350 = 650) does not replace the exact sum, but it can catch a miss such as 5,312.

Remember

Carry when a place makes 10 or more. Estimate to see whether an answer is reasonable, not as a second official sum.

Watch me

Why do we write 2 and carry 1 when the ones are 5 + 7, instead of writing 12 in the ones place?

  1. 12 ones are 1 ten and 2 ones.
  2. The 1 ten belongs in the tens place, so we carry it. Writing 12 in the ones place would break the places.

Together

A student gets 522 for 285 + 347, having forgotten a carry. How does an estimate show that 522 is not reasonable?

Hint 1

300 + 350 = 650, or 280 + 350 = 630.

Hint 2

522 is about 100 less than that estimate, so a carry was likely missed.

Check

These defend carrying and estimating.

  1. What does an estimate not claim, in this lesson?
  2. Why is a part-whole bar a good picture for 285 + 347?

Practice

  1. Write a short argument that 285 + 347 = 632, naming at least one carry.
  2. An invented total of 632 cards (labeled invented) is a whole. Why is it not a NASA count?

Spanish · Numbers once to quince

15 minutes · Method: hear and count. Instruction in English.

Learn

Today extends the numbers from Level 1 with once to quince (11 to 15). Uno–diez may be reused as a recap, not a first look.

Word How to say it Meaning
uno OO-noh one
diez dyess ten
once OHN-seh eleven
doce DOH-seh twelve
trece TREH-seh thirteen
catorce kah-TOR-seh fourteen
quince KEEN-seh fifteen

The guide counts invented paper sticks or marks, labeled invented, and says the Spanish number. Do not use these as official rocket counts.

Instructions are in English. Hear clips use es-MX. Greetings and numbers 1–10 from Level 1 may be reused, not retaught as a first look. If a student began at Level 2, the guide may first use Level 1 Days 1–2 Spanish for hola and uno–diez, then continue here.

Remember

Once, doce, trece, catorce, quince are 11 to 15. Uno–diez are already known. Hear and point first.

Watch me

The guide holds 12 marks and says doce. What should the student do, and what does doce mean?

  1. Point to the twelve, or show twelve.
  2. Twelve.

Together

What is the English of quince, and of once?

Hint 1

Quince: fifteen.

Hint 2

Once: eleven.

Check

These check 11 to 15.

  1. Say once, doce, trece in order, with the English.
  2. What is 14 in Spanish, and 15?

Exercises

  1. Hear catorce and quince. Give the English.
  2. Count from diez to quince in Spanish.

Latin · Longa and brevis

15 minutes · Method: two opposite captions for a shadow. Original sentences only.

Learn

Today adds longa (long) and brevis (short) to yesterday’s umbra.

Word How to say it Meaning
longa LONG-ah long
brevis BREH-wis short
umbra OOM-brah a shadow

The Hear-word button uses an Italian voice as an approximation of Latin, not the speech of ancient Rome.

Original captions:

  • Umbra longa est. — The shadow is long.
  • Umbra brevis est. — The shadow is short.

Use them with two drawings, not as official lengths. Alta from yesterday may be reused when a picture shows a high / tall shadow.

All Latin here is original. Explanations are in English. The Hear-word button uses an Italian voice as an approximation of Latin, not the speech of ancient Rome. Vowels are pure; c like k; v like English w.

Remember

Longa: long. Brevis: short. Umbra longa est and Umbra brevis est are original captions for long and short shadows.

Watch me

A short midday-shadow drawing is shown. Which caption matches?

  1. Umbra brevis est.

Together

What is the English of Umbra longa est?

Hint 1

The shadow is long.

Check

These check longa and brevis.

  1. Give the English of Umbra brevis est.
  2. What does umbra mean?

Exercises

  1. Hear longa and brevis. Give a caption for a long shadow.
  2. Write one original caption for a short shadow.

Notebook: a part-whole bar for 285 + 347

60 minutes · draw first, then label, then write · How the notebook works

Learn

Draw first: a part-whole bar for 285 and 347, and the column addition with carries visible. Label parts, whole, carry. Then write from stems.

Word bank: part, whole, carry, ten, hundred, estimate, six hundred thirty-two.

Stems: 1. I carried 1 ten because … 2. I could have written 12 in the ones place, but … 3. My estimate was near 650, so …

End with 285 + 347 = 632 and the estimate, and one “what next” question.

Remember

Draw the bar and the columns first. The because-clause should name the 12 ones as 1 ten and 2 ones.

Watch me

Finish: “I carried 1 ten because …”

  1. A model: “… because 5 + 7 = 12 ones, which is 1 ten and 2 ones.” Other complete finishes accepted.

Together

Finish the but-stem about writing 12 in the ones place.

Hint 1

12 ones are a ten and 2 ones; the ten belongs in the tens place.

Check

These check the bar and the carry in writing.

  1. What is the whole, and what were the parts?
  2. What estimate is enough for the so-stem?

Practice

  1. Finish all three stems in writing.
  2. Write one “what next” question about a missing part.
