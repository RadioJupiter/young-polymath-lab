Core lesson: arrays, and 4 rows of 9

45 minutes · concrete → pictorial → abstract

Learn

An array is equal rows, with the same number in each row. 4 rows of 9 is 4 × 9, or 9 + 9 + 9 + 9.

Count in 9s: 9, 18, 27, 36. The product is 36. You may also see 9 rows of 4: 9 × 4 = 36. 4 × 9 = 9 × 4.

Concrete: four strips of 9 cubes, or four rows of 9 counters (invented classroom marks, labeled invented). Then the picture, then 4 × 9 = 36.

A week has 7 days, so this 4-by-9 array is not a picture of four weeks. Four weeks would be 4 × 7 = 28 days. Do not mix the 9s in the array with the 7 days of a week.

The length of the month used in this level is from the NASA Moon fact sheet: the Moon’s synodic period is 29.53 days, taught here as about 29½ days. https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html This level does not invent a finer official length, and does not begin the sidereal month or Moon phases as fractions (those belong later). Classroom bag, rock, and array counts are invented and labeled invented. They are not official NASA counts. A week of 7 days is a calendar fact used here, not a NASA orbital period.

Remember

4 rows of 9 is 4 × 9 = 36, and also 9 × 4 = 36. Count in 9s, or use an array. Classroom arrays are invented. Four weeks are 28 days, a different fact.

Watch me

How many are in 4 rows of 9, and how can you see it as a multiplication?

  1. 36.
  2. 4 × 9, or 9 × 4.

Together

What is 4 × 7, and what does that count if each 7 is a week?

Hint 1

Four 7s: 28.

Hint 2

Four weeks are 28 days, a calendar count.

Check

These read arrays.

  1. How many in 4 rows of 9?
  2. Find 4 × 7.

Practice

  1. Draw or describe 3 rows of 9, and give the product.
  2. Why is 4 rows of 9 not 4 + 9 = 13?

Challenge problems

25 minutes · try every one; being stuck for a while is part of the work

Learn

If 4 rows of 9 are 36, then 5 rows of 9 are one more 9: 36 + 9 = 45. 3 rows of 9 are one 9 less: 36 − 9 = 27.

Remember

Add or take one row to move from a known array to a nearby one.

Watch me

Using 4 rows of 9 = 36, how many are in 5 rows of 9?

  1. 36 + 9 = 45.

Together

How many are in 3 rows of 9?

Hint 1

One row less than 36.

Hint 2

27.

Check

These stretch the array.

  1. Find 6 × 9, from 4 × 9 if it helps.
  2. Find 4 × 10.

Practice

  1. An invented array has 4 rows and 36 marks in all, with equal rows. How many in each row?
  2. Why is 4 × 9 not 49?

Math circle: rows, and what the 9 is not

20 minutes · one rich problem, solved and then explained

Learn

In 4 rows of 9, the 9 is how many are in each row, not “day 9” and not a NASA figure. The 36 is the total of the array, an invented classroom count unless a different source is named.

Remember

Name what each number in the array stands for. Do not turn a classroom array into an official sky count.

Watch me

In 4 × 9 = 36, what does the 4 stand for, what does the 9 stand for, and what does the 36 stand for?

  1. 4 is the number of equal rows (or groups).
  2. 9 is how many in each row. 36 is the total.

Together

Why must this 4-by-9 array not be called a NASA count of days or of rocks on the Moon?

Hint 1

The marks are invented classroom marks, labeled invented.

Hint 2

NASA’s month in this level is about 29½ days, a different, sourced figure.

Check

These keep the array honest.

  1. What is 4 × 9, and what is 4 × 7?
  2. Which of those two products can name four weeks?

Practice

  1. Write a short argument that 4 rows of 9 are 36.
  2. Name one calendar fact and one NASA fact already mentioned this level, if any, and keep them labeled. (If the NASA month has not yet been used as a number in your sitting, say so, and keep the array labeled invented.)

Spanish · Fila, and cuatro and nueve

15 minutes · Method: point at rows. Instruction in English.

Learn

Today names fila (row) with cuatro and nueve, used with an invented 4-by-9 picture, labeled invented. Do not give 36 as an official NASA count in Spanish.

Word How to say it Meaning
fila FEE-lah row
cuatro KWAH-troh four
nueve NWEH-beh nine
grupos GROO-pohs groups

The guide points at one row and says una fila, then at four rows: cuatro filas. Instruction stays in English.

Instructions are in English. Hear clips use es-MX. Words from Levels 1 and 2 may be reused, not retaught as a first look. If a student began at Level 3, the guide may first use earlier foundation Spanish, then continue here.

Remember

Fila: row. Cuatro: four. Nueve: nine. Point at rows first. The product stays in the mathematics block, in English.

Watch me

The guide points at one row of the array and says una fila. What should the student do?

  1. Point at one row.

Together

What is the English of cuatro filas, and of nueve?

Hint 1

Four rows.

Hint 2

Nine.

Check

These check fila and the numbers.

  1. Give the English of fila, cuatro, and nueve.
  2. Hear grupos and fila. Give the English of each.

Exercises

  1. Say the Spanish for “row” and for “four.”
  2. Why is 36 not given as a Spanish official count?

Word How to say it Meaning
quattuor KWAT-too-or four
novem NO-wem nine
septem SEP-tem seven

The Hear-word button uses an Italian voice as an approximation of Latin, not the speech of ancient Rome.

Original captions:

  • Quattuor. — Four.
  • Novem. — Nine.

Do not write 4 × 9 as a Latin official product. The multiplication stays in English.

All Latin here is original. Explanations are in English. The Hear-word button uses an Italian voice as an approximation of Latin, not the speech of ancient Rome. Vowels are pure; c like k; v like English w.

Notebook: 4 rows of 9, and four weeks

60 minutes · draw first, then label, then write · How the notebook works

Learn

Draw first: a 4-by-9 array labeled invented, and a separate 4-by-7 note for four weeks (28 days, calendar). Then write from stems.

Word bank: array, rows, thirty-six, weeks, twenty-eight, invented, calendar.

Stems: 1. 4 rows of 9 are 36 because … 2. I could have written 4 + 9 = 13 for the array, but … 3. Four weeks are 28 days, so …

End with 4 × 9 = 36 and 4 × 7 = 28, clearly distinguished, and one “what next” question.

Remember

Draw both pictures first. The so-stem should keep 28 as calendar weeks, not as a NASA month.

Watch me

Finish: “4 rows of 9 are 36 because …”

  1. A model: “… because four 9s are 36, or 4 × 9.” Other complete finishes accepted.

Together

Finish the but-stem about 4 + 9.

Hint 1

13 is the sum, not four 9s.

Check

These check the two products in writing.

  1. Which product is the array, and which is four weeks?
  2. What label stays on the 4-by-9 marks?

Practice

  1. Finish all three stems in writing.
  2. Write one “what next” question about sharing a total into equal rows.
