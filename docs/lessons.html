Units 1–3 cover Level 4 (big numbers), Level 5 (multiplication and division), and Level 6 (fractions, Moon phases, and gravity on other worlds) of the level map. Each day has mental math, a core lesson, challenge problems, a math circle, a short Spanish lesson, a short Latin lesson, and a notebook project (with writing stems and a printable worksheet set).

Math circle topics so far: systematic counting, logic grids, magic squares, parity, the balance puzzle, counting pairs, the pigeonhole principle, divisibility tests, sand-timer puzzles, colouring invariants.
