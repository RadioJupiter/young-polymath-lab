Math circle: why tallies group in fives

Check. 1. Ten is larger, so a half-finished group can be a lot of marks to count. Five is small enough to see at a glance, and two fives make ten. 2. A scatter is easy to miscount, and a later reader cannot see the groups.

  1. Count the marks again, together if needed, and agree on the record. The total has to match the marks.
