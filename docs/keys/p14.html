Math circle: find the equal group first, and keep two lengths distinct

Check. 1. 24 ÷ 3 = 8, then 5 × 8 = 40. 2. 28 days (four weeks) and about 29½ days (NASA synodic period, from 29.53).

  1. Each of the three bags holds 8, so each of five like bags holds 8, and 5 × 8 = 40. The counts are invented.
  2. 28 is 4 × 7, four calendar weeks. The NASA synodic period is 29.53 days, taught as about 29½, which is 1½ days more than 28.
