Math circle: the planet puzzle

Puzzle 1. Jupiter is in place 4, so Venus is in place 3. Earth is not at an end, so Earth is in place 2, and Mars takes place 1 (to the left of Venus, as required). Mars, Earth, Venus, Jupiter.

Puzzle 2. C is Wednesday. A and B are on two days in a row that avoid Wednesday: Monday–Tuesday or Thursday–Friday. If A were Monday, nothing could be before it, but E must be. So A is Thursday and B Friday. D and E take Monday and Tuesday, and D is before E: D Monday, E Tuesday.
