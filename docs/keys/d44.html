Challenge problems: expected versus observed, and a fair reading

Check. 1. Expected: about 5 heads. Observed: 7 heads. A short run can differ from what is expected. 2. The yearly mean is an average of that year’s daily totals. One afternoon is only one look, and it can be different.

  1. 10 cm, because it sits on the mark. “About 10 cm” is still fair if you are speaking of the same whole-centimeter scale, but the mark already supports 10 cm.

Math circle: a short run is not a proof, and “about” is not a dodge

Check. 1. Because a short run is not locked to the exact half. About 5 is what the fair chance points to, not a promise of exactly 5. 2. The parts are not equal chances, so “1 out of 4” would name a fair split the spinner does not have.

  1. They can say “about 15,” or “14 to 16,” and then recount if they can. They should not invent a more exact official rate, such as 15.00, from the disagreement.
