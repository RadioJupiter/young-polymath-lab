Math circle: why both sides change together

Check. 1. 5n means five equal parts that together make 20. To find one part, both sides have to be split the same way, or the equality is lost. 2. If you changed only one side, putting the number back will not give the original right-hand side.

  1. The left becomes n, the right stays 9, and n = 9 would fail the check: 9 − 4 is 5, not 9. The true n is 13.
  2. A note that the circle is a reason to talk through, not a reward.
