Math circle: the pigeonhole principle

  1. Three. Two might be one of each colour; a third must match one of them.
  2. There are 12 months (the "pigeonholes") and 13 people (the "pigeons"). If every month had at most one person, there would be at most 12 people. So some month has two. ∎
  3. Yes. Every whole number leaves a remainder of 0, 1 or 2 when divided by 3: three pigeonholes. Four numbers means two share a remainder, and the difference of two numbers with the same remainder divides exactly by 3 (the remainders cancel). ∎

Notebook: a clock for Mars

After 3 sols: 3 × 40 = 120 minutes = 2 hours behind. After 9 sols: 360 minutes = 6 hours. (A precise sol is 24 h 39 min 35 s.) Any design that is explained clearly with one real advantage and one real drawback is a good answer. The three sentences should connect (they are about the same design).
