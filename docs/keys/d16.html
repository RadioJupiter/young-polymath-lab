Challenge problems: mixed units, and a comparison with Earth

Check. 1. Rename: 3 m 10 cm = 2 m 110 cm, then 2 m 110 cm − 1 m 80 cm = 1 m 30 cm. (Or 310 − 180 = 130 cm = 1 m 30 cm.) 2. 2,500 m. Mars’s equatorial diameter is about 6,792 km, which is 6,792,000 m, so the invented path is a tiny fraction of the planet’s width — as a classroom path should be. The path length is invented; the diameter is not.

  1. 5 m 20 cm.
  2. 2,000 mm = 2 m; 180 cm = 1.8 m; 1.5 m. Order: 1.5 m, 180 cm, 2,000 mm.
  3. 3,396 km. (That happens to match the equatorial radius on the fact sheet, which is a useful check, not a new source.)

  5. 7 m.

Math circle: same length, different names

Check. 1. For example, 3 m and 300 cm. 2. A kilometer is a larger unit than a meter, so one kilometer is longer than one meter. A length given in kilometers is not automatically longer than a length given in meters (0.2 km is shorter than 500 m).

  1. Yes: 80 cm is 0.8 m, which is longer than 0.5 m.
  2. 6,800 km, which is a rounding of about 6,792 km to the nearest hundred (and should be labeled if we treat it as Mars’s diameter).
  3. Any clear sentence that keeps the two ideas apart.

Spanish · ¿Cuánto mide? Meters and centimeters

  1. el metro; el centímetro.
  2. A correct pair, e.g. ¿Cuánto mide? Mide tres metros.
