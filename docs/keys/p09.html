Math circle: a shadow, a stick, and what we may claim

Check. 1. The Sun’s height in the sky (higher near midday). The stick’s height stays the same. 2. A comparison bar.

  1. When the Sun is lower, light hits the stick more from the side, so the shadow stretches farther along the ground. When the Sun is higher, the shadow shrinks. No official length is needed for that reason.
  2. NASA: Saturn V 111 m (363 feet) on the student page cited. Invented: 63 m and 18 m rockets, or 80 cm and 20 cm shadows. The lesson names the source or the invented label.
