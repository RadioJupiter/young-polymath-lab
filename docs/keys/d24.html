Math circle: why a sideways throw can look like a curve

Check. 1. C = πd is the distance around a circle. An orbit is a motion in time (a path being traveled), and it may not be a perfect circle. Even in a circular model, C alone does not give a speed or a period unless a time is also given, and we are not inventing one. 2. Any honest “does not prove”: a numerical speed, a period, or that every orbit is a circle.

  1. Any clear sentence that this is a first look, not a computed mission figure.
