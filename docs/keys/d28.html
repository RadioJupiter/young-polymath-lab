Math circle: why we line up the point

Check. 1. The 4 in 0.4, and the 2 in 0.25. 2. We would be adding different places as if they were the same, and we would get a wrong sum such as 0.29.

  1. The helpful zero restates an amount we already have (0.4 = 0.40). Writing 6,792.00 km would claim a hundredth of a kilometer we did not measure.
  2. 5 tenths + 5 tenths = 10 tenths = 1; 50 hundredths + 50 hundredths = 100 hundredths = 1. Both ways agree.
  3. Only the written places in the sum; the numbers themselves stay 1.36 and 0.4 (or 0.40).
  4. Today’s core is addition and subtraction. × 10 is a place-value shift (each digit moves one place to the left), which we used only in the mental warm-up.
  5. …ones must sit under ones and tenths under tenths…
