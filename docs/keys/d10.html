Check. 1. 2 days and 2 hours (48 + 2).

  1. 384,400 km at 1,000 km/h takes 384.4 hours. 384 ÷ 24 = 16 exactly. So 16 full days, then 0.4 of an hour (24 minutes) more, landing on the 17th day.
  2. Largest: put the biggest digits in the hundreds places: 6 and 5 in the hundreds, 4 and 3 in the tens, 2 and 1 in the ones: 600 + 500 + 40 + 30 + 2 + 1 = 1,173 (for example 642 + 531). Smallest: 1 and 2 in the hundreds, 3 and 4 in the tens, 5 and 6 in the ones: 381 (for example 135 + 246).
  3. 1 h 20 min = 80 minutes = 4,800 seconds. 4,800 × 300,000 = 1,440,000,000 km: about 1.4 billion km, roughly as far as Saturn is from the Sun.

Math circle: the broken chessboard

Check. 1. Opposite corners do not share a side, and they are the same color; a domino covers two neighbouring squares of opposite colors.

It is impossible. Opposite corners are the same color, so removing them leaves 8 squares of one color and only 6 of the other. Every domino, wherever it is placed, covers one dark and one light square. Seven dominoes would cover 7 of each, but the board has 8 and 6. The same argument works on the 8 × 8 board (32 and 30 remain after removing two same-coloured corners, but 31 dominoes would need 31 of each).
