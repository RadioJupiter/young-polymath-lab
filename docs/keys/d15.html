Unit check.

Part A. 1. A bar of 4 equal parts with 3 shaded. 4 is how many equal parts the whole was cut into; 3 is how many of those parts are shaded. 2. The disk looks 1/2 lit, not 1/4. “Quarter” means about a quarter of the way through the cycle, not a quarter of the disk. 3. 7⅜ days (29½ ÷ 4 = 7¼ + ⅛ = 7⅜). 4. New: 0. Full: 1. 5. 3/12 = 1/4; 9/12 = 3/4. (The 12-day month is a model.)

Part B. 6. 3/6 and 6/12. 7. 1/2, because 3/6 ÷ 3/3 = 1/2, or because a 6-part bar with 3 shaded is half. 8. 1/6 of 30 = 5 units of pull. The mass does not change. 9. No: 2/5 = 20/50, not 19/50. 2/5 is a slightly high estimate for Mars (about 0.380) and must be labeled as an estimate. 10. Divide top and bottom by 2, or mark 2 parts of a 12-part bar and 1 part of a 6-part bar of the same length.

Part C. 11. 5/8 (already simplest). 12. 4/10 = 2/5. 13. 1/2 = 3/6; 3/6 + 1/6 = 4/6 = 2/3. 14. 2/3 = 4/6; 4/6 − 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2. 15. 1/3 = 2/6; 2/6 + 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 full, so 1/2 is still empty. The bar should show equal sixths (or a 6-part bar).
