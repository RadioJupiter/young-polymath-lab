Challenge problems

Check. 1. 1,000. 990 is 9 hundreds and 9 tens; one more ten makes 10 tens, which make 1 more hundred, so 10 hundreds: 1,000. 2. 493. (5 hundreds, 0 tens, 3 ones: you regroup 1 hundred as 10 tens, then take 1 ten, leaving 4 hundreds, 9 tens, and 3 ones.)

  1. The 24 hours is the whole cycle of day and night together, for most places, as Earth spins once. Night is the part of that cycle when a place faces away from the Sun, not the whole 24 hours.
  2. 28 + 5 = 33, then 33 + 30 = 63 (or 28 + 30 = 58, then 58 + 5 = 63).
