Challenge problems

Check. 1. 720 is greater: it has 7 hundreds and 2 tens; 702 has 7 hundreds and 0 tens. The tens place decides once the hundreds are equal. 2. 20 stars (5 × 4, or 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4).

  1. 10 tens make 1 more hundred, so this is 2 hundreds: 200.
  2. 200 is 2 hundreds. 10 less is 1 hundred and 9 tens: 190. (Or: 200 − 10 = 190.)

Math circle: counting without missing any

Check. 1. They use a plan that visits each star once, so they are counting the same set. 2. Only if you know how many are in the hidden row (or that the rows are equal and you know how many rows and how many in a visible row). Guessing the hidden row is not a count.

  1. 2 rows of 6, or 6 columns of 2: both 12. Other invented equal-group pictures accepted if both counts agree and the picture is labeled invented.
  2. The real sky is not an invented classroom picture, and this lesson does not invent an official star count. We count a set we can plan for.

Spanish · Hola, adiós, and two actions

Check. 1. Goodbye; when leaving, or at the end of the sitting. 2. Hola is a greeting (hello). Levántate asks you to stand up.

  1. Siéntate: sit down. Levántate: stand up.
  2. The method is listen and act first. Speaking can come when the student is ready.
