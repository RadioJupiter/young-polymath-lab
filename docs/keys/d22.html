Core lesson: polygons, and the angles of a triangle

Check. 1. 80°. 2. 120°, invented pad.

  1. 85°, because 80 + 95 + 100 = 275, and 360 − 275 = 85.
  2. A polygon has straight sides. A crater rim is a curve (and not a perfect circle either).
  3. No. A non-square rectangle has four right angles, but adjacent sides are not equal, so it is not regular.
  4. Sum (8 − 2) × 180° = 1,080°. Each interior angle, if regular, is 1,080° ÷ 8 = 135°.
  5. A labeled triangle whose three angles add to 180°.

A crater rim is not a polygon, and 120° is the interior angle of a regular hexagon, which is a different kind of figure. The sentence would treat a curve as a straight-sided regular hexagon.

  1. Third angle 40°. Not equilateral, because the angles are not all equal (so the sides cannot all be equal).

Math circle: why the angle sum is (n − 2) × 180°

Check. 1. A triangle is already one triangle, so n − 2 = 1, and the sum is 180°, which we already had. 2. A crater outline is not a polygon, so it does not split into (n − 2) straight-sided triangles in this way.

  1. Any clear sentence that the (n − 2) is a triangle count.
