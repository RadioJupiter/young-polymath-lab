Remember

A line graph shows change over time. The yearly means in this lesson come from SILSO’s SN_y_tot_V2.0 file. In this window, 2019 is lowest and 2024 is highest; 2025 is lower than 2024. A rise already on the graph is not a promise that the next year will rise too, and a future official mean is not invented.

1. Between 2019 and 2021, did the yearly mean rise or fall, and what are the two means?

2. About how much greater is the 2023 mean than the 2022 mean? (125.5 − 83.2.)

3. In the same SILSO series, 2008 is 4.2 and 2019 is 3.6. What spacing do those two low years show, and what do they not prove by themselves?

4. Why are the invented meteor counts from Days 41–42 kept out of this sunspot graph?
