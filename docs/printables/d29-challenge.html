Challenge problems: ranges and means, and a freezer that is not a planet

Unit 6 · Day 29 · Negative numbers, and planet temperatures · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Count through 0 to find a difference. Keep means, ranges, and invented examples labeled.

1. How many degrees warmer is Mercury’s mean (167°C) than Earth’s mean (15°C)?

2. Which is colder, about −173°C (Moon, darkness, NASA Science) or −180°C (Mercury, night side, NASA Science)?

3. Earth’s mean is 15°C; Mars can be as high as 20°C. Does that mean Mars is always warmer than Earth? Say why, using the mean and the range.

4. How many degrees warmer is about 127°C (Moon, full Sun) than the Moon’s mean of −20°C?

5. A friend says Venus is hotter than Mercury “because 467 is a bigger number than 430.” What two kinds of figure is that mixing, and what comparison did this lesson actually compute?

6. An invented classroom thermometer reads −5°C, then later 8°C. How many degrees did it rise? Label the readings invented.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
