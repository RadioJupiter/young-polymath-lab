Remember

A division and its multiplication are two views of the same equal groups. The check is the groups put back as a whole.

1. What multiplication checks 36 ÷ 4 = 9?

2. What division belongs with 7 × 8 = 56, besides 56 ÷ 7?

3. Write a short argument that 54 ÷ 6 = 9, with a check.

4. Why are the 54 counters labeled invented?
