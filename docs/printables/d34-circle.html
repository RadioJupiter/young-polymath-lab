Math circle: a percent needs a named whole

Unit 7 · Day 34 · Percent as per hundred · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Percent means per hundred. 25% = 1/4 = 0.25. The whole must be named. We will not invent a NASA percent.

1. Write 10% as a fraction and as a decimal.

2. What is 100% of 36?

3. Give one reason 50% of 18 is 9, not 50.

4. Why does a homemade “75% of the Moon is rock, from NASA” fail today’s honesty habit?

5. How does 25% sit beside 0.25 and 1/4?

6. If two invented bags are both “30% red,” do they have the same number of red items? What else do you need to know?
