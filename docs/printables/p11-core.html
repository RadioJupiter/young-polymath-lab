Core lesson: equal groups, and 7 × 8

Level 3 · Day 11 · Equal groups, and 7 × 8 · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

7 × 8 means 7 groups of 8, or 8 groups of 7, and equals 56. An array shows equal rows. 7 × 8 = 8 × 7. Classroom arrays are invented and labeled invented. Eight weeks are 56 days, a calendar count, not a NASA month.

1. Find 7 × 8 and 8 × 7.

2. How many days are in eight weeks?

3. Draw or describe an array for 4 × 5, and give the product.

4. A student writes 7 × 8 = 15 because 7 + 8 = 15. What was mixed?
