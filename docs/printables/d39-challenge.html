Remember

Undo a rule in reverse order. For the lever, mass × distance equals mass × distance, in this model, when it balances.

1. The rule is n → 4n + 2. Output 22: what was the input?

2. A labeled invented lever has 3 at 8 units on the left, and n at 6 units on the right, and it balances. Find n.

3. The rule is n → 2(n + 5). Input 3: what is the output? Output 18: what was the input?

4. If a friend doubles only the mass on one side of a balanced invented lever, and leaves the distances as they were, what happens in this model?

5. Why is “I multiplied mass by mass, and distance by distance” a miss in this model?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
