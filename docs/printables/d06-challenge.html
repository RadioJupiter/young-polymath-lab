Remember

Hours = days × 24. Numbers in both every-3 and every-4 lists are multiples of 12. Factor pairs of n give the possible arrays.

1. How many hours are in 5 days?

2. Is 24 a multiple of both 3 and 4?

3. How many hours are in 30 days? In a 365-day year?

4. Sixty satellites in a ring are numbered 1 to 60. Every third one carries a camera, and every fourth one carries a radio. How many carry both?

5. A rectangle of dots contains exactly 36 dots, arranged in equal rows. List every possible shape (rows × columns).
