Mental math warm-up: renaming a number as equal parts

Unit 3 · Day 12 · One sixth as strong · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Equal amounts can have different names: 1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6. Finding half, then checking that two quarters give the same answer, is a first test of that idea.

1. What is 2/4 of 16? What is 1/2 of 16?

2. What is 4/8 of 24? What is 1/2 of 24?

3. 1/2 of 40 and 3/6 of 40.

4. 2/4 of 100 and 1/2 of 100.

5. A quarter of 32, and two eighths of 32. Are they equal?

6. Name two fractions that both mean half of a 12-unit bar.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
