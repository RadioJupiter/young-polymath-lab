Core lesson: place value to a million

Unit 1 · Day 1 · How far is the Moon? · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Ten of one disc make one of the next disc. Each place is worth ten times the place to its right. ×10 moves every digit one place left. A number bond splits a number into two parts that add to the whole.

1. Show 3,475 (the Moon's width in km) on a chart. What is 10 × 3,475?

2. Write a number bond: 384,400 = 80,000 + ?

3. Show 12,756 on a place-value chart (or with discs). Then show 10 × 12,756 on a new chart. What happened to each digit?

4. The Moon is 384,400 km away. A probe is 10 times as far. How far is the probe?

5. The Sun is about 1,391,400 km across. Write a number bond: 1,391,400 = 1,000,000 + ?. Then find 10 × 1,391,400.

6. 38,440 × 10 and 38,440 × 100. What changes on the chart each time?
