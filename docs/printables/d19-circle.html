Math circle: a liter is a cube, not a guess

Unit 4 · Day 19 · A box, a liter, and a labeled habitat · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

1 L = 1,000 cm³ = a 10 cm cube. 1 m³ = 1,000 L. The shape of a bottle can change; the official relation does not.

1. What cube, in centimeters, has volume 8 L? (Think 8,000 cm³.)

2. A 5 cm cube: volume in cm³ and as a fraction of a liter.

3. How many 10 cm cubes fill a 1 m cube? (1 m = 10 of those 10 cm edges.)

4. Write one sentence a guide could post: liter, official, cube.

5. Why did we not use a Mars “air liter” number today?
