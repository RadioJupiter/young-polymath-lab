Core lesson: angles, and a turn

Unit 5 · Day 21 · Angles, and a turn · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Angle = a turn, in degrees. Full turn 360°, straight 180°, right 90°. Acute: between 0° and 90°. Obtuse: between 90° and 180°. Straight line: the two angles add to 180°. Around a point: they add to 360°. Vertically opposite angles are equal.

1. An angle of 50° sits on a straight line with another angle. Find the other angle, and name both (acute or obtuse).

2. Three equal angles meet around a point, with no leftover space. How large is each?

3. Two lines cross. One angle is 125°. Find the other three angles.

4. Can a right angle sit on a straight line with an acute angle? Say why, with degrees.

5. A door opens 90°, then 30° more in the same direction. What is the total turn, and is the total acute, right, or obtuse?

6. Draw two lines that cross, mark one angle 40°, and fill in the other three.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
