Remember

Full turn 360°. Straight angle 180°. Right angle 90°. Half of 180° is 90°. Half of 90° is 45°.

1. Half of a full turn, in degrees.

2. Three right angles, in degrees.

3. How many right angles make a full turn?

4. A 45° angle is what fraction of a right angle?

5. What is 360° − 90°?

6. Is 80° a right angle, an acute angle, or an obtuse angle? (Acute means less than 90° but more than 0°; obtuse means more than 90° and less than 180°. Those names are used from the core lesson onward; if this warm-up comes first, say 80° is less than a right angle.)
