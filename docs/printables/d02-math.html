Practice

1. Order these diameters from smallest to largest: Mars 6,792 km; Mercury 4,879 km; Venus 12,104 km; Earth 12,756 km.

2. Round Earth's diameter (12,756 km) to the nearest ten, the nearest hundred and the nearest thousand.

3. Round the Moon's distance (384,400 km) to the nearest ten thousand and the nearest hundred thousand.

4. Which is closer to 50,000: Neptune's diameter (49,528 km) or Uranus's (51,118 km)?

5. How many planets are closer than 200 million km to the Sun? How many are farther than 1,000 million?
