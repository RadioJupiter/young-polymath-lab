Unit 1 · Day 5 · Walking the Solar System · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practise only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

A short story is just sentences you already know, one after another. La luna es blanca y grande — the moon is white and large. Y means and.

1. What does y mean in blanca y grande?

2. What does Ana mira el cielo mean?

3. Put the five story sentences in order (the guide may cut them up, or just read them out of order for pointing).

4. Point and say as much of the story as you can. Pointing through it silently is acceptable if speech is not ready yet.

5. Copy the sentence you like best.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
