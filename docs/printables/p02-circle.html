Remember

A complete list needs a plan, like yesterday’s counting plan. List in order: 0 + 10, 1 + 9, 2 + 8, …

1. Why must you say whether 8 + 2 and 2 + 8 count as one bond or two sentences?

2. Is 10 + 0 a bond of 10? Why or why not?

3. List every bond of 5, counting swaps as the same pair.

4. A student lists 1 + 9, 2 + 8, 3 + 7, 4 + 6 and says “that is all.” What was missed, if pairs include 0 and 5?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
