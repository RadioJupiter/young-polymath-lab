Handwriting and writing scaffold

Copy the model sentence on the first line. Then finish the stems in your best handwriting. Say each sentence aloud before you write it.

Model sentence (copy on the line below):

The Moon is far away.

Your sentences:

The Earth in the model is about ________.

The Moon in the model is about ________.

The gap between them is about ________.

What surprised me was ________.

For the guide: Draw first. Label from the word bank. The student says each sentence before writing. If handwriting is a struggle, write the student's own words in light pencil to trace or copy. After the draft is down, tidy spelling from the word bank. Do not correct every error in the first writing.
