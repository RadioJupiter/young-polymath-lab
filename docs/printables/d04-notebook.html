Notebook · The Moon journal begins

1. Draw first. Draw the Moon as it looks tonight (or the last time it was seen), large, with any markings you can really see. Do not look at the Sun.

2. Label from the word bank. Point, say the word, then write it on the drawing.

Word bank — Moon · date · time · sky · cloudy · shape · crescent · half · full · prediction · week

3. Write. Say each sentence aloud, then write it on the lines.

Tonight the Moon looks ________.

I saw it ________.

In one week the Moon will look ________ because ________.

4. Numbers and questions. Add the date and time. Prepare a row of boxes for the next week's evening drawings (you may use the back of this sheet).

For the guide: If it is cloudy, draw the last Moon actually seen and write 'cloudy tonight'. Help set up the week's boxes.
