Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practise only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

AB + BA = 11(A + B). The sum 1 + 2 + … + n is n pairs of (n+1), then divide by 2, or n(n+1)/2.

1. Using the pairing method, what is 1 + 2 + … + 20?

2. A two-digit number AB plus its reverse BA equals 121. Find every possibility. (AB means the digits A and B, not A × B.)

3. Add up every whole number from 1 to 100, without adding them one at a time.

4. Mercury, Venus and Mars have diameters 4,879 km, 12,104 km and 6,792 km. Add them in the order that is easiest for you, and explain why you chose it.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
