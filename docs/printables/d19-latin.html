Remember

Aqua in cistā est. in + ablative for place where (in cistā). nōn est for ‘is not.’ No liters in the Latin. Italian-voice Hear, labeled as an approximation.

1. Write: “The box is tall, and water is in the box.”

2. Correct: Aqua in cista est if the lesson wants the ablative cistā.

3. Write a nōn-sentence about water and a wall or a box.

4. Read the sentences aloud twice.

5. Draw a box with water, labeled Aqua in cistā est.

6. Write one new sentence with alta or parva and cista.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
