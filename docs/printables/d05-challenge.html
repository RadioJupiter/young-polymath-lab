Unit 1 · Day 5 · Walking the Solar System · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

model cm = real km ÷ 70,000. Time = distance ÷ speed. Keep the units matching.

1. At 1 m/s, how many seconds to walk 120 m of the model?

2. The nearest star beyond the Sun, Proxima Centauri, is about 40 trillion km away (40,000,000,000,000 km). In our model (1 cm for 70,000 km), about how far away would it be? Give your answer in kilometers.

3. In the model, Neptune is 645 m from the Sun. How long would it take to walk there at 1 meter per second? Give the answer in minutes and seconds.

4. Light takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach Earth from the Sun. In the model, Earth is 21.4 m away. How fast does "model light" move, in centimeters per second?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
