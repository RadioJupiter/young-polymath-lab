Remember

1 day = 24 × 60 × 60 seconds. You can compare products by rewriting, not only by computing both.

1. How many seconds are in 5 minutes?

2. How many seconds are there in one day? Show your steps.

3. Find the missing digit: 3? × 4 = 148.

4. Which is larger, 25 × 48 or 24 × 50? Decide without multiplying either one out, then check.

5. If Earth moves about 30 km every second, roughly how far does it travel in one day? (Use your answer to question 1 and round sensibly.)
