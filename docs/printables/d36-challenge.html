Unit 8 · Day 36 · A letter for an unknown · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

A letter still stands for one number in a problem. Draw a bar, write an equation, then check.

1. A whole of n is made of 14 and 9 and 7. Find n.

2. 3 + n = n + 3. What does this tell you about n, and what does it not tell you?

3. n + n = 18. Find n.

4. An invented shelf holds 40 books. 15 are on the first pile and n are on the second. The two piles together are the whole shelf. Find n.

5. Why can we use the letter n again in this new shelf problem, even though n was 15 in an earlier problem?
