Remember

Nominative does the looking (puella, puer). Accusative is looked at (lūnam, stēllās). Puella lūnam spectat. Puer stēllās spectat. An adjective on the object matches it: lūnam clāram.

1. Write: The girl looks at the stars.

2. Correct: Puella lūna spectat.

3. Translate: The boy looks at the moon. The girl looks at the bright stars.

4. Write two original sentences, one with lūnam and one with stēllās.

5. Correct: Puer stēllae spectat.
