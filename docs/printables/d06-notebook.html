1. Draw first. Draw Earth as a circle 12.8 cm across. Draw the ISS orbit only 4 mm above the surface. (Optional: a small Moon-journal box in a corner.)

3. Write. Say each sentence aloud, then write it on the lines.

The Station looks slow because ________.

The Station looks slow but ________.

The Station looks slow so ________.
