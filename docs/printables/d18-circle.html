Math circle: why the units are square

Unit 4 · Day 18 · Covering a floor · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

cm is a length unit; cm² is an area unit (a 1-by-1 square). Name the unit, or the number is unfinished.

1. Draw 1 cm² and 1 cm, and write a caption for each.

2. A 1 m by 1 m invented tile: area in m² and in cm².

3. Why can two shapes have the same area and different perimeters? Give one pair you can actually draw (for example, a 6 by 2 rectangle and a 4 by 3 rectangle).

4. Write one sentence a guide could post: length unit, area unit.

5. What would be wrong with calling Mars’s diameter 6,792 km²?
