Unit 2 · Day 8 · Years on other worlds · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practise only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

365 = 52 × 7 + 1. If N leaves remainder 1 when divided by a, then a divides N − 1.

1. A date is a Friday this year. Next year is not a leap year. What day is the same date next year?

2. This year, a date falls on a Tuesday. Next year is not a leap year. On what day will the same date fall next year? Explain using division.

3. Find the smallest whole number greater than 1 that leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 2, by 3, by 4, by 5 and by 6.

4. A year on Saturn lasts about 10,747 Earth days. About how many Earth years is that? (Use 365 days in a year; first estimate, then calculate to one decimal place or as "a bit more than ___".)

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
