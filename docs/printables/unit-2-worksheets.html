Practice

1. 365 ÷ 7: how many weeks and days are in a 365-day year?

2. Mercury's year is 88 Earth days. How many Mercury years pass in 352 Earth days?

3. 1,000 ÷ 8

4. A mission packs 240 meals for 8 days. How many meals per day?

6. Roughly how many full Moons happen in a 365-day year? Use 30 days for the cycle, then think about whether the true 29.5 changes the answer.

Unit 2 · Day 10 · Mission planning day

Math practice · Polymath Lab · d10 · math

NameDate

Work in pencil. Use the boxes for working. Do not open the answer key until you have finished.

Mental math

Say how you got each answer, then write it.

1. 384,400 + 15,600

2. 1,000,000 − 1

3. 16 × 25

4. 4,800 ÷ 6

5. 99 × 7

6. 3 hours = how many minutes?

7. Round 142,984 to the nearest thousand.

8. 72,000 ÷ 9

Practice

Part A: number

1. Write 2,867,000,000 in words.

2. What is the value of the 6 in 1,632,450?

3. Round 49,528 to the nearest thousand and to the nearest ten thousand.

4. Put in order, smallest first: 120,536; 51,118; 142,984; 49,528.

5. What number is 100,000 less than 1,042,000?

Part B: calculation

6. 68,457 + 24,986

7. 90,000 − 37,465

8. 248 × 6

9. 35 × 24 (show your area model)

10. 952 ÷ 7

Part C: problems (draw a bar model for each)

11. The Moon is about 384,400 km away. A spacecraft has 129,850 km still to go. How far has it travelled?

12. A rover drives 125 m each day. How far does it drive in 4 weeks?

13. Radio messages take 500 seconds to come from a probe. How many minutes and seconds is that?

14. A planet's year lasts 225 Earth days (close to Venus's 224.7). How many full weeks is that, and how many days left over?

15. Two moons have diameters that add up to 5,000 km. One is 1,200 km wider than the other. How wide is each?
