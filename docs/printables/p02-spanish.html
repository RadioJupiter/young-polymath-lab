Remember

Uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez are 1 to 10. Hear and point first. Instruction stays in English.

1. Say, in order, uno to cinco, and give the English.

2. What is diez in English, and what is 10 in Spanish?

3. Hear seis, siete, and nueve. Give the English of each.

4. Point and say the Spanish for 3 and for 8.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
