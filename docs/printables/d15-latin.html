Remember

Nox est clāra. Ubi est lūna? Lūna est in caelō. Puella lūnam spectat. Puer stēllās spectat. Adjectives match: lūnam clāram, stēllās clārās, stellae clārae.

1. Write: The boy looks at the stars.

2. Correct: Ubi sunt lūna? Puella lūna spectat.

3. Retell the story in Latin, with the book closed as soon as you can.

4. Translate into Latin: The bright stars are in the sky. The boy looks at the moon.

5. Write two original sentences: one ubi question and answer, and one with spectat and an accusative.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
