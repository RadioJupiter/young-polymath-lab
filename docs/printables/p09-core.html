Remember

Part-whole for a total; comparison for a difference. A longer shadow goes with a lower Sun; a shorter shadow with a higher Sun. Classroom shadow lengths (80 cm and 20 cm) are invented and labeled invented. The stick’s height stays the same.

1. Invented rockets 63 m and 45 m: find the difference and the stacked whole, and name the bar for each.

2. Invented shadows 80 cm and 20 cm: find how much shorter the midday shadow is.

3. A student adds 80 + 20 and calls that the stick’s height. What was mixed?

4. Why must you not look at the real Sun to do this observation?
