Remember

Use one unit at a time. Invented tanks stay labeled. 1 L = 1,000 cm³; 1 m³ = 1,000 L.

1. A 5 m by 2 m by 0.4 m invented cistern: volume in m³ and in liters.

2. A cuboid has volume 60 cm³, length 5 cm, and width 4 cm. Find the height.

3. Why is “the crew needs 45,000 L of water” a misuse of today’s 45 m³?

4. 1,500 cm³ in liters, and 2.5 L in cm³.

5. A 1 m by 1 m by 1 m cube next to a 100 cm by 100 cm by 100 cm cube: same volume or not?

6. Write a two-sentence warning to put at the top of a design page: what is sourced, and what is invented.
