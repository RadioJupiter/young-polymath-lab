Printables

The language of instruction is English. These sheets are for Units 1 and 2. Print them on Letter paper, in black and white. Use the Print button. Answer keys are on separate pages and are not in the combined PDFs.

Each day has five student sheets: math practice, the notebook project (drawing box, word bank, stems and lines), a handwriting and writing scaffold, Spanish, and Latin.
