Core lesson: adding big numbers and the part-whole model

Unit 1 · Day 3 · Adding up the worlds · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Part-whole: the parts add to the whole. Line up the places. Add ones first. Ten in a column is traded for 1 in the next column (the same trade you make with discs).

1. What is 1,276 + 475? Trade tens of a place for one of the next.

2. In a part-whole bar, Earth 12,756 km and Moon 3,475 km are the parts. What is the whole?

3. Mercury (4,879 km across) and Mars (6,792 km) side by side: how wide? Is that wider or narrower than Earth (12,756 km)?

4. A spacecraft flies to the Moon and back: 384,400 km each way. How far is the round trip?

5. A probe travelled 125,600 km on its first day and 98,750 km on its second. Draw the bar model and find the total.

6. Venus (12,104 km), Earth (12,756 km) and Mars (6,792 km) side by side: how wide?
