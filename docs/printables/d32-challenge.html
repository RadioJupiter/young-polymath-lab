Challenge problems: a missing length, and two honest scales

Unit 7 · Day 32 · Equivalent ratios, and a scale · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Same methods as the core. Two scales can both be right. Labels stay honest.

1. 1 : 8 = 5 : □.

2. An invented map is 1 cm : 10 km. A real 35 km road is how many centimeters on the map? Label the map invented.

3. Why is 2 : 3 = 4 : 6, but 2 : 3 is not equal to 2 : 6?

4. About 150,000,000 ÷ 384,400 is about 390. NASA says the Moon is “roughly 400 times” closer than the Sun. How can both be honest?

5. At 1 cm : 1,000 km, a length of 2.5 cm stands for how many kilometers?

6. A friend wants to draw the 3.84 m gap as 4 cm “so it fits.” What is lost if the gap no longer uses the same scale as the sizes?
