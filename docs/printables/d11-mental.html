Remember

Half means ÷2 (two equal parts). A quarter means ÷4, or half of a half. Use number bonds: 80 = 40 + 40; 80 = 20 + 20 + 20 + 20.

1. What is half of 120?

2. What is a quarter of 40?

3. Half of 16, half of 800, half of 5,000.

4. A quarter of 100, a quarter of 48, a quarter of 200.

5. Three quarters of 40 (three of the four equal parts).

6. Half of 90, then a quarter of that answer.
