Remember

Last digit even ↔ ÷2. Last digit 0 or 5 ↔ ÷5. Last two digits ÷4 ↔ the number ÷4. Digit sum ÷3 ↔ the number ÷3.

1. Does 7,215 divide by 3? By 5?

2. Does 1,116 divide by 4?

3. Test 384,400 for division by 2, 3, 4, 5 and 9.

4. Test 1,391,400 (the Sun's diameter in km) for 3 and 9.

5. Investigate: pick any three-digit number, and subtract the sum of its digits from it. Try five numbers. What do you notice about the answers? Can you explain why?
