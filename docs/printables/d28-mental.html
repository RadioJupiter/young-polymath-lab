Remember

Add tenths to tenths and hundredths to hundredths. × 10 moves every digit one place to the left. 10 tenths make 1 one.

1. 0.6 + 0.6.

2. 0.04 × 10.

3. 1.2 − 0.5 (think of 12 tenths minus 5 tenths).

4. 3.14 × 10, and say what the 4 stands for in the answer.
