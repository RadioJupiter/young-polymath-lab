Challenge problems: a missing angle, and a turn that is not a length

Unit 5 · Day 21 · Angles, and a turn · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Straight line 180°, around a point 360°, vertically opposite equal. Degrees are a turn, not a length.

1. Two lines cross. The adjacent (next-door) angles are in the ratio 1 to 3. Find all four angles.

2. A telescope is turned 40°, then turned back 15° the other way. What is the net turn from the start, and why is that not a distance on the Moon?

3. Can three acute angles add to a straight angle? Give an example, or say why not.

4. If someone writes “the angle is 90 km,” what has gone wrong?

5. Draw a straight line and a ray that cuts it, with one angle 2/5 of 180°. Find both angles.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
