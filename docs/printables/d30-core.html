Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Name the place of each digit. Line up the point. Count through 0 for a temperature difference. Label means, ranges, and invented classroom numbers. Four out of five or better on each part is mastery; this is not a score, a badge, or a reward.

1. Write, as a heading in your book: Part A place value and comparing, Part B adding and subtracting decimals, Part C negative numbers and temperatures. Then begin Part A when the guide says.

2. When you finish a part, do not add extra credit items; the five questions are the part.

3. Write 0.07 and 0.70 in ones, tenths, and hundredths, and say which is greater.

4. Write 3 ones, 2 tenths, and 5 hundredths as a decimal.

5. Write 0.4 as hundredths, and as a fraction of a whole.

6. Order from least to greatest: 0.8, 0.08, 0.75.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
