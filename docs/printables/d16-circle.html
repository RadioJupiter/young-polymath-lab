Math circle: same length, different names

Unit 4 · Day 16 · Meters, centimeters, and a planet's size · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

The size of the unit is not the same as the size of the length. 2 m = 200 cm, even though a meter is a larger unit than a centimeter.

1. Give a pair of names for the same length, one in meters and one in centimeters.

2. Why is “kilometers are always longer than meters” a confused sentence, and how would you repair it?

3. Can a length of 80 cm be longer than a length of 0.5 m? Show the names in one unit.

4. If a drawing of Mars is 6.8 cm across, and the card says “1 cm stands for 1,000 km,” what diameter is being modeled, and is that a rounding?

5. Write one sentence that a guide could post: unit, not length / length, not unit.
