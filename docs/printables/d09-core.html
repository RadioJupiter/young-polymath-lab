Unit 2 · Day 9 · Messages at the speed of light · one-lesson review

Remember

Time = distance ÷ speed. Light travels at about 300,000 km per second. Moon light-time ≈ 384,400 ÷ 300,000 ≈ 1.3 s. Sun light-time ≈ 150,000,000 ÷ 300,000 = 500 s = 8 min 20 s.

1. A radio message travels 600,000 km at 300,000 km/s. How many seconds is that?

2. Change 90 seconds into minutes and seconds.

3. How long does light take to travel 900,000 km?

4. A radio message travels at the speed of light. When Mars is 60 million km from Earth, how long does a message take to arrive? Give minutes and seconds.

5. Record your pendulum results in a table: length, time for 10 swings, time for one swing.

6. When the length was made 4 times longer (25 cm → 100 cm), what happened to the time of one swing?
