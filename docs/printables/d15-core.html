Remember

A fraction is equal parts of one whole. Equivalent fractions name the same amount. Like denominators: add or subtract numerators. Unlike denominators: rename first. Mass does not change on the Moon; the pull is about 1/6.

1. Write 1/2 as sixths.

2. What is 2/6 + 1/6, in sixths and in simplest form?

3. Draw a bar in 4 equal parts and shade 3/4. What does the 4 mean, and what does the 3 mean?

4. At first quarter, what fraction of the near-side disk is lit, as we see it? Why is the phase still called “quarter”?

5. The synodic month is about 29½ days. What is 1/4 of 29½ days?

6. At new Moon, what fraction of the near-side disk is lit, as we see it? At full Moon?
