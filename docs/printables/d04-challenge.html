Unit 1 · Day 4 · How much farther? · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

For a sum S and a difference D: the smaller is (S − D) ÷ 2; the larger is the smaller plus D.

1. Two lengths add to 5,000 km and one is 1,200 km longer. How long is the shorter?

2. I think of a number. I subtract 2,375 and get 6,880. What was my number?

3. Two numbers add up to 10,000, and one is 1,250 more than the other. Find them. (Draw two bars.)

4. A rocket's fuel tank holds 9,000 liters. After the first burn, 3,875 liters are left; after the second burn, 1,290 liters are left. How much fuel did the second burn use? How much did both burns use together?
