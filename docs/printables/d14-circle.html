Remember

Any common denominator works; the least one is often easiest. The trap is adding numerators and denominators (1/4 + 1/6 = 2/10), which is not addition of amounts.

1. 1/4 + 1/6 = ? Use twelfths.

2. Is 8 a common denominator for 1/2 and 1/8? Is it a common denominator for 1/3 and 1/8?

3. 1/8 + 1/4, two ways if you wish (eighths, or sixteenths then rename).

4. A two-sentence argument: I must not add 1/4 + 1/6 as 2/10 because ________. The true sum is ________.

5. Find a common denominator for 3 and 8, and compute 2/3 − 1/8.

6. Draw a 12-part bar that shows both 1/4 and 1/6, then their sum.
