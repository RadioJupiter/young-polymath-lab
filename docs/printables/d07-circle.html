Math circle: the pigeonhole principle

Unit 2 · Day 7 · Riding the Earth around the Sun · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practise only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

To be sure of two of the same kind when there are k kinds, take k + 1 (one of each, then one more).

1. To be sure of two gloves of the same colour (red or blue), how many must you take?

2. A crew of 13: why do at least two share a birth month?

3. In a dark cupboard there are many red gloves and many blue gloves, all mixed up. How many must you take out, without looking, to be sure of having two of the same colour?

4. A crew has 13 people. Prove that at least two of them have birthdays in the same month.

5. Is it true that among any 4 whole numbers, two of them have a difference that divides exactly by 3? Explain.
