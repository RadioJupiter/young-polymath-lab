Handwriting and writing scaffold

Copy the model. Then finish the same opening three ways: because, but, so.

Model sentence (copy on the line below):

The Sun is huge because it is much wider than Earth.

Your sentences:

Earth is drawn ________.

Jupiter is about ________ times as wide as Earth.

The whole Sun at this scale would be ________.

The Sun is huge because ________.

The Sun is huge but ________.

The Sun is huge so ________.

For the guide: New step: because / but / so. Ask 'because why?' rather than dictating the ending. The student speaks each full sentence first. After the draft is down, tidy spelling from the word bank. Do not correct every error in the first writing.
