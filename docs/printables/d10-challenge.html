Remember

Hours ÷ 24 gives days and leftover hours. Largest sum: biggest digits in the highest places of both addends. distance = 300,000 km/s × seconds.

1. A trip of 50 hours: how many whole days and leftover hours?

2. A spacecraft leaves Earth and travels 1,000 km every hour. How many whole days would it take to reach the Moon (384,400 km)? How many hours are left over in the last day?

3. The digits 1 to 6 are each used once to make two three-digit numbers. What is the largest possible sum? The smallest?

4. A probe sends a photo that takes 1 hour 20 minutes to arrive at the speed of light (300,000 km per second). About how far away is the probe?
