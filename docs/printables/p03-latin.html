Latin · Nōn est

Level 1 · Day 3 · The other part of the bond · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Nōn est means “is not.” Sōl nōn est is said when the picture is not the Sun. Est still means “is” when the picture matches.

1. Write or say a true caption for a drawing of the Sun, and a true “is not” caption for the same drawing if someone said it was the Moon.

2. What does salvē mean, still?

3. Hear nōn and lūna. Give the English of Lūna nōn est.

4. Point at a star drawing and say two Latin captions: one that matches, and one that uses nōn about the Sun.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
