Math circle: proving two fractions are the same amount

Unit 3 · Day 12 · One sixth as strong · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Equivalent fractions can be proved with a matching bar, or by multiplying or dividing top and bottom by the same number. A matching length is the test, not a similar-looking scribble.

1. Can 3/6 = 2/4? Prove it or show they both equal 1/2.

2. Why is multiplying top and bottom by 0 not allowed?

3. Prove 2/6 = 1/3 with a 6-part bar and with the rule.

4. A friend says 2/5 = 3/6 because ‘you add 1 on the top and 1 on the bottom.’ Show that this is not a legal step.

5. Write a two-sentence argument: These are equivalent because ________. They would not be equivalent if ________.

6. On one 12-part bar, mark 1/2, 2/4, 3/6, and 6/12. What do you notice?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
