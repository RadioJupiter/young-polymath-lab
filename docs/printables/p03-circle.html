Math circle: a missing part, and a reason

Level 1 · Day 3 · The other part of the bond · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

One whole and one part name the other part. That is why a subtraction fact has one answer.

1. If the whole is 10 and one part is 3, what is the other part, and how do you know you have them all?

2. Can 15 have two different partners for the part 8, if we stay with whole numbers?

3. Write a short argument: why 8 + 7 and 7 + 8 give the same whole, but 15 − 8 and 15 − 7 do not give the same part.

4. Invent a whole and a part (labeled invented) and name the other part two ways: as a subtraction and as a missing-addend sentence.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
