Core lesson: division and remainders

Unit 2 · Day 8 · Years on other worlds · one-lesson review

Remember

Short division: share each place in turn, from the left. What will not share equally is written as a remainder on the next place (or recorded at the end). Check by multiplying the quotient by the divisor and adding any remainder.

1. What is 48 ÷ 4? Check by multiplying.

2. 84 ÷ 6, by short division.

3. 365 ÷ 7: how many weeks and days are in a 365-day year?

4. Mercury's year is 88 Earth days. How many Mercury years pass in 352 Earth days?

5. 1,000 ÷ 8

6. A mission packs 240 meals for 8 days. How many meals per day?
