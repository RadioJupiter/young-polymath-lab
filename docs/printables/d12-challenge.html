Challenge problems: gravity on Mars, and a careful estimate

Unit 3 · Day 12 · One sixth as strong · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Mars gravity ≈ 0.380 ≈ 19/50 of Earth’s. 2/5 = 0.4 is a slightly high estimate. Equivalent fractions must be exact; estimates must be labeled.

1. Write 0.380 as a fraction of 1000 (380/1000) and simplify as far as you can.

2. If Earth’s pull is 50 units, about how many units on Mars (use 19/50)?

3. Which is larger, 19/50 or 2/5? How much larger, as a fraction of 50?

4. A student writes 1/6 = 1/5 because ‘both are small.’ What has gone wrong? Show 1/6 and 1/5 on bars of equal length, or with a common denominator.

5. Earth pull 30 units. About how many units on the Moon (1/6) and on Mars (19/50 of 30)? For Mars you may leave an unsimplified form and then a mixed or whole-number rounding, labeled as rounded.

6. (Stretch.) 19/50 and 38/100: are they equivalent? Why does that matter for 0.38 and 0.380?
