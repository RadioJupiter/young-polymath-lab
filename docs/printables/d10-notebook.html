Notebook · Design a mission

1. Draw first. Draw your spacecraft from two views (side and front), with labels, the way Leonardo da Vinci drew machines.

2. Label from the word bank. Point, say the word, then write it on the drawing.

Word bank — spacecraft · destination · Moon · Mars · Jupiter · distance · light-time · travel · power · food · message · problem · question

3. Write. Say each sentence aloud, then write it on the lines.

Topic sentence: This mission goes to ________.

Notes (not full sentences):

Closing sentence:

Short paragraph, written from the outline only:

4. Numbers and questions. Write the distance, the light-time for messages, and a travel time at a speed you choose (show the division). List three problems and one idea for each if they did not all fit in the paragraph.

For the guide: The design must be the student's. Help with numbers already in the unit. If handwriting is tiring after the unit check, scribe the paragraph exactly as spoken, for the student to copy.
