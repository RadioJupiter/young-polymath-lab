Handwriting and writing scaffold

Copy the model. Then expand the kernel: first add where or when, then add because.

Model sentence (copy on the line below):

The walk was surprising because space is emptier than a picture shows.

Your sentences:

The part of the walk that surprised me most was ________.

Books do not draw the Solar System to scale because ________.

Expand: The walk was surprising. — Add where or when, then why.

For the guide: Ask the expansion questions one at a time. The student says the longer sentence before writing it. After the draft is down, tidy spelling from the word bank. Do not correct every error in the first writing.
