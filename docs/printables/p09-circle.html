Remember

Observation plus labeled invented measurements. Do not turn classroom lengths into official figures.

1. What changes from morning to midday: the stick’s height, or the Sun’s height in the sky?

2. Which bar compares 80 cm and 20 cm?

3. Write a short argument that a longer shadow goes with a lower Sun, without inventing an official length.

4. Name one NASA figure and one invented figure from this level, and say how you tell them apart.
