Remember

Perimeter = distance around. Rectangle: 2 × (L + W). L-shape: walk every outer edge (the pictured one is 26 cm). Invented habitat measures must be labeled invented. Do not count inner edges as if they were outer.

1. A 9 cm by 4 cm rectangle: find the perimeter.

2. A 10 cm by 6 cm rectangle has a 3 cm by 3 cm square cut from the top-right corner. Sketch, walk the outline, and find the perimeter.

3. A square of side 12 m: perimeter. (Invented habitat courtyard, labeled invented.)

4. Why is “add all the numbers you see on the diagram” sometimes too much, for an L-shape?

5. A 7 cm by 2 cm rectangle and a 7 cm by 2 cm rectangle joined along a 2 cm side to make one long rectangle: what is the new perimeter, and what happened to the joined sides?

6. Walk around a 1 m by 80 cm rectangle, in centimeters.
