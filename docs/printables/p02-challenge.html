Remember

When you add to a number like 18, you can still make a ten with the ones, then add any new ten you made.

1. Find 28 + 5 by making ten with the ones.

2. Why is 8 + 7 not equal to 87?

3. Find 17 + 8 two ways (start from 17, or start from 8) and show that both give the same total.

4. An invented desk has 9 pencils and 7 more are added. How many then? Label the numbers invented.
