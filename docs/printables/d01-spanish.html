Remember

Hola is hello; adiós is goodbye. A command such as mira or levántate is something you can do at once. The stressed syllable is the one written in capital letters in 'How to say it'.

1. What does gracias mean?

2. If the guide says mira, what do you do?

3. When the guide says Siéntate, Levántate, Mira, Para, Señala el lápiz, do the action (no talking required).

4. Point to the picture and say Hola.

5. Answer, with a name: ¿Cómo te llamas?
