Remember

A letter stands for one unknown in a problem. Do the same to both sides, then check. A pattern is a rule you can say and use. Larger distance from the Sun, longer year, from the fact sheet — no invented formula or orbital speed. For the lever, mass × distance equals mass × distance, in this model, when it balances. Four out of five or better on each part is mastery; this is not a score, a badge, or a reward.

1. Write, as a heading in your book: Part A unknowns and equations, Part B patterns and rules, Part C the balance and the lever. Then begin Part A when the guide says.

2. When you finish a part, do not add extra credit items; the five questions are the part.

3. A bar has a whole of 22, with parts n and 9. Write the equation and find n.

4. n − 7 = 11. Find n, and say what you did to both sides.

5. 5n = 40. Find n, and check.

6. 2n + 6 = 18. Find n, and check.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
