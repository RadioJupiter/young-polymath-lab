Core lesson: tenths, hundredths, and decimal place value

Unit 6 · Day 26 · Tenths, hundredths, and place value · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

0.1 = 1/10. 0.01 = 1/100. After the ones: decimal point, tenths, hundredths. Each place is ten times smaller than the place on its left. 0.4 is 4 tenths; 0.04 is 4 hundredths. Invented classroom lengths stay labeled.

1. Which is greater, 0.4 or 0.04? Say what each digit stands for.

2. Write 5 tenths and 2 hundredths as a decimal.

3. Write 0.6 as a fraction of a whole, in tenths.

4. In 3.14 (the labeled approximation of π), what does the 1 stand for, and what does the 4 stand for?

5. Shade 0.08 on a hundred-square, or say how many small squares that is.

6. An invented classroom rod is 2.05 m long. How many ones, tenths, and hundredths is that? Label the length invented.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
