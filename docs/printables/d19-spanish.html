Remember

hay agua / no hay agua; necesitamos agua / aire. Instruction in English. No invented Spanish science figures.

1. Write a question with ¿Hay…? and a short answer.

2. Write one sentence with necesitamos.

3. Hear agua and aire twice, then use each.

4. A three-line scene: a question, hay or no hay, and necesitamos.

5. Why might you keep “240 liters” in English on the page, if you mention it at all?

6. Add ¿Cuánto mide… only if you are measuring a tank in the math, not as a new Spanish number drill.
