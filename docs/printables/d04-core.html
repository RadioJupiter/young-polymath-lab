Core lesson: subtraction and the comparison model

Unit 1 · Day 4 · How much farther? · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practise only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Trading down is carrying run backwards: one disc is ten of the next smaller disc. Comparison bars: when two amounts are compared, the difference is the extra part of the longer bar. Take-away bars: the whole minus a part leaves the other part.

1. 700 − 698, by counting on.

2. A tank holds 9,000 liters and 3,875 have been used. How much is left? Which bar is this — part-whole or comparison?

3. How much wider is Jupiter (142,984 km) than Saturn (120,536 km)?

4. Mars is about 228 million km from the Sun and Earth about 150 million km (rounded). How much farther from the Sun is Mars? Why is your answer only approximate?

5. What must be added to 384,400 to make 1,000,000?

6. Saturn takes about 10,747 days to go once around the Sun; Jupiter takes 4,331. How many more days does Saturn take? Draw the comparison model.
