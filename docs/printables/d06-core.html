Remember

The area model: split each factor into tens and ones (or other useful parts), find the four (or two) smaller rectangles, and add. This is the same as the distributive law.

1. Split 15 × 6 as (10 + 5) × 6 and add the parts.

2. Using 16 orbits a day, how many orbits does the ISS make in a week?

3. Draw the area model for 23 × 4 and find the product.

4. A planetarium has 7 rows of 12 seats. How many seats?

5. How many hours are in a week?

6. A made-up space station has 6 solar wings, each with 8 panels. How many panels?
