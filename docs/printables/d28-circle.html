Math circle: why we line up the point

Unit 6 · Day 28 · Adding and subtracting decimals · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Line up the point because we are lining up place value. A helpful zero does not change the amount. The last digit is not always the same place.

1. In 0.4 + 0.25, which digits are the tenths?

2. What goes wrong if we “add 4 and 25 and put a point in front”?

3. Give one reason a helpful zero is not the same thing as inventing a more precise NASA size.

4. Compute 0.5 + 0.5 two ways: as tenths, and as hundredths (0.50 + 0.50).

5. Does lining up the point change 1.36 or 0.4, or only their written places in the sum?

6. Why was 3.14 × 10 not today’s kind of problem, and what place-value idea did it still use?
