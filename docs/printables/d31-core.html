Core lesson: ratio as a comparison

Unit 7 · Day 31 · Ratio as a comparison · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

A ratio a : b compares two quantities of the same kind, in that order, by equal parts. 2 : 3 is not 3 : 2. Together, 2 : 3 has 5 parts. Divide or multiply both sides by the same number to write an equivalent ratio. Invented mixes stay labeled. Earth 12,756 km to the Moon 3,475 km is a little less than 4 : 1, not exactly 4 : 1.

1. Write the ratio of 3 to 5, and the ratio of 5 to 3. Are they the same comparison?

2. Simplify 10 : 15.

3. Simplify 4 : 10.

4. A 1 : 4 bar has 5 parts. If each part is 3 cm, how long is each bar? (Invented classroom lengths, labeled invented.)

5. Earth’s fact-sheet diameter is 12,756 km and the Moon’s is 3,475 km. Why is “exactly 4 : 1” a little high?

6. A friend writes 6 : 9 = 7 : 10 because “I added 1 to both.” What is the sound way to simplify 6 : 9?
