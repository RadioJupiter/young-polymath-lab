Core lesson: bar graphs and pictographs

Unit 9 · Day 42 · Bar graphs and pictographs · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

A bar graph needs an even scale, and every bar is read from 0 against that scale. A pictograph needs a key that says what one picture stands for. The same invented counts can be shown both ways. Invented counts stay labeled invented.

1. How much greater is Watch 2 than Watch 1 on the bar graph?

2. A new pictograph uses the key “one square = 5.” A row shows 3 squares. What count does that row show?

3. Why would a scale that went 0, 2, 4, 10, 12 be a problem on a bar graph?

4. Why is a pictograph without a key not ready to be read?
