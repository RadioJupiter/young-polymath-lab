Remember

Equal amounts stay equal. A trailing zero can still mark a finer reading, but it cannot invent a measurement. A sourced mean and a sourced range stay labeled. This circle is not a second score.

1. Name one sourced mean from this unit and one invented classroom number.

2. Why is a mean not automatically “the temperature there”?

3. Are 0.4 and 0.40 the same point on a number line?

4. What must stay labeled when we compute 3.14 − 0.04?

5. Why did we line up the decimal point, not the last digits?

6. Venus’s mean is hotter than Mercury’s mean, from the same fact sheet. What comparison did we not invent a cause for, beyond that fact?
