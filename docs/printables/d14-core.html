Core lesson: unlike denominators, and 1/6 again

Unit 3 · Day 14 · Different parts, one bar · one-lesson review

Remember

Unlike denominators: rename to a common denominator, then add or subtract as like fractions. 1/2 + 1/6 = 4/6 = 2/3; 1/2 − 1/6 = 2/6 = 1/3; 1/3 + 1/6 = 1/2. A 6-part bar shows halves and sixths. Mass still does not change on the Moon.

1. 1/3 + 1/6 = ? Show the renaming.

2. 2/3 − 1/2 = ? (A common denominator of 6 works: 4/6 − 3/6.)

3. 1/2 + 1/6, 1/2 − 1/6, and 1/3 + 1/6, each in simplest form.

4. 1/4 + 1/2 and 3/4 − 1/6 (for 1/6, a common denominator of 12: 9/12 − 2/12).

5. A 6-part gravity bar: Earth’s pull 6/6, Moon 1/6. Write the difference, and write the ratio of Moon to Earth.

6. A student writes 1/2 + 1/6 = 2/8. Correct the error and say what rule was skipped.
