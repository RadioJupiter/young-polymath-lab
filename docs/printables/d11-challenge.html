Challenge problems: fractions of a cycle

Unit 3 · Day 11 · The Moon's quarters · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

A fraction of 29½ is an equal-parts problem. The sidereal month is shorter (about 27⅓ days) than the synodic month (about 29½ days); the difference is a fact to notice, not a formula to invent today.

1. What is half of 27⅓ days? (Half of 27 is 13½; half of ⅓ is ⅙. Combine them.)

2. Why are the two ‘months’ not the same length, in one or two sentences of your own?

3. A classroom model uses a 12-day ‘month’ (this is a model, not the real 29½-day month). What fraction of that model has passed after 3 days? After 9 days?

4. On the real 29½-day month, about how many days is 1/2 of the way (new to full)?

5. If a drawing of the disk at first quarter is shaded 1/4, what is wrong, and what should be shaded?

6. (Stretch.) The sidereal month is about 27.32 days and the synodic about 29.53 days (NASA, unrounded here). Subtract and say about how many days they differ, rounding to the nearest tenth.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
