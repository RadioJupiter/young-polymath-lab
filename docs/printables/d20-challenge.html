Remember

Same tools: place-value shifts, P = 2(L+W), A = L×W, half a rectangle, V = LWH, 1 L = 1,000 cm³. Invented plans stay labeled. No extra credit.

1. A right triangle with legs 6 m and 3 m is an invented annex. Find its area, then, if the “height” of the room over that floor is 2.5 m, the annex volume (a triangular prism: area of the triangle times the length along which it is extruded — here, the 2.5 m wall height, if the triangle is the floor).

2. A 1.2 L bottle, official conversion: how many cm³? Is that bottle a sourced crew ration? (Say no, and say why.)

3. Why is 65 m³ in Watch not a NASA volume?

4. Order these from smallest to largest volume: 2 L, 500 cm³, 0.003 m³. (Use one unit.)

5. Write one sentence that could head a design page: sourced facts listed, invented numbers listed.
