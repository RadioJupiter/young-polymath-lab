Remember

Ones, tens, hundreds: ten of one place make one of the next place. 247 is 2 hundreds, 4 tens, and 7 ones. 1,000 is ten hundreds. Classroom star counts are invented and labeled invented. Count in a fixed order so you do not miss any.

1. What is 305 in hundreds, tens, and ones?

2. What number is 6 hundreds, 0 tens, and 4 ones?

3. Write 1,000 as hundreds.

4. An invented picture has 2 rows of 5 stars. How many stars? Why must you still count in order?
