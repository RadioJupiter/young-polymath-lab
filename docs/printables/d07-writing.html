Handwriting and writing scaffold

Copy the model. Then write three connected sentences about your design: what it is, one advantage, one problem.

Model sentence (copy on the line below):

A day on Mars is a little longer than a day on Earth.

Your sentences:

My Mars clock ________.

One advantage is ________.

One problem is ________.

For the guide: The design must be the student's. Oral rehearsal of the three sentences first. Number them 1, 2, 3 if that helps. After the draft is down, tidy spelling from the word bank. Do not correct every error in the first writing.
