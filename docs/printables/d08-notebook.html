Notebook · Predicting the full Moon

1. Draw first. Draw the Sun on one side, Earth in the middle, and the Moon in four positions around Earth. Shade the half of each Moon that faces away from the Sun. Mark which position is a full Moon as seen from Earth.

2. Label from the word bank. Point, say the word, then write it on the drawing.

Word bank — Sun · Earth · Moon · full · new · quarter · reflects · sunlight · shade · prediction · opposite

3. Write. Say each sentence aloud, then write it on the lines.

A full Moon happens when ________.

My predicted date for the next full Moon is ________.

Finish all three, with the same opening:

A full Moon happens because ________.

A full Moon happens but ________.

A full Moon happens so ________.

4. Numbers and questions. Add 29 or 30 days to the last full Moon you recorded (or looked up) and write the predicted date.

For the guide: Help the student shade the unlit half first, then ask which position looks full from Earth. Do not answer first.
