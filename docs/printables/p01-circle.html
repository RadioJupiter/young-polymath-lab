Remember

A count you can trust has a plan. “I just looked and saw 12” is not a plan.

1. Why can two careful people get the same total for the same invented picture?

2. If a row is hidden, can you still be sure of the total? What must you know?

3. Invent (and label invented) a 2-by-6 star picture. Count it two ways and show that both ways agree.

4. Why is “count the real sky tonight and get a number for all the stars” not this lesson’s job?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
