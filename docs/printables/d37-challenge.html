Challenge problems: two moves, still a balance

Unit 8 · Day 37 · A balance, and keeping both sides equal · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Do the same to both sides, one move at a time, then check.

1. 4n + 6 = 26. Find n.

2. n is on both sides: n + 7 = 2n + 1. Find n.

3. A labeled invented left pan holds n + 9. The right pan holds 20, and the balance is level. Find n.

4. Why is “I moved 5 from one side only, and then divided” a miss?

5. 2(n + 3) = 14. Find n. (2(n + 3) means two lots of n + 3.)

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
