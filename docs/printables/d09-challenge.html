Unit 2 · Day 9 · Messages at the speed of light · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practise only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

distance = speed × time. Build a large product in stages and keep the units.

1. How many swings in an hour, at 2 seconds per swing?

2. If a 1 m pendulum takes about 2 seconds per swing, how many swings does it make in a minute? In an hour?

3. A light-year is the distance light travels in a year. Using 300,000 km per second and a 365-day year, work it out step by step: per minute, per hour, per day, per year.

4. Radio signals to a rover on Mars can take anywhere from about 3 to about 22 minutes, depending on where the planets are. If a driver on Earth sees the rover approach a rock and sends "stop!", what problem is there? How do engineers solve it?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
