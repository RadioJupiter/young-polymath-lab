Unit 1 · Day 5 · Walking the Solar System · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

A scale keeps every ratio the same. If 1 cm stands for 70,000 km, a real length L km becomes L ÷ 70,000 centimeters. Distances in the model subtract just as they do in space.

1. If 1 cm stands for 70,000 km, what real distance is 10 cm in the model?

2. In the model, Earth is at 21.4 m and Jupiter at 111 m from the Sun. How far is it from Earth to Jupiter?

3. In the model, how far is it from Earth to Mars (when they are lined up on the same side of the Sun)?

4. How many times wider than Earth is Jupiter in the model? Is that the same as in space?

5. If your pace is 50 cm, how many paces is it from the Sun to Earth in the model? To Jupiter? ### Unit check (do alone, 15 minutes)

6. Saturn is about 1,432 million km from the Sun. Write this in kilometers. Then write a number bond: 1,432,000,000 = 1,000,000,000 + ?.
