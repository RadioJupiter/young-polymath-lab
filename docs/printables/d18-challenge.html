Unit 4 · Day 18 · Covering a floor · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Split or subtract rectangles. Label invented plans as invented. Keep area in square units.

1. The invented 6 m by 3 m module has area 18 m². A right-triangle annex with legs 6 m and 3 m is attached. What is the annex area, and the combined area?

2. A rectangle’s area is 35 cm² and one side is 7 cm. Find the other side, and then the perimeter.

3. Why does a 2 m doorway usually not change the floor area the way it can change a wall-length question?

4. Two invented rooms, 4 m by 3 m and 2 m by 5 m: total area, and a sketch.

5. A right triangle is half of a 10 cm by 6 cm rectangle. Find its area.

6. If 1 m² is 10,000 cm² (because 1 m = 100 cm, and 100 × 100 = 10,000), write 2 m² in square centimeters. (A first look at area-unit shifts.)
