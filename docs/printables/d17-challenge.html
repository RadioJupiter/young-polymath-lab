Challenge problems: a fence around a module, and missing sides

Unit 4 · Day 17 · Distance around a pad · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Name the path you are measuring: the full outline, or the outline minus a door. Invented module sizes must stay labeled invented.

1. A rectangle has perimeter 20 m and length 6 m. Find the width.

2. An invented 5 m by 5 m square pad with a 1 m by 1 m square bite from one corner: is the perimeter 20 m, or more, or less? Explain with a sketch.

3. Why might “how much fencing?” need an extra fact besides the perimeter of the floor?

4. A 12 cm by 5 cm rectangle: perimeter in centimeters and in meters.

5. If every outer edge of an L is labeled except one, and the walk must close, how do you find the missing edge? (Use a horizontal or vertical sum.)

6. Write a two-sentence caption for the 8 m by 4 m module, including the word invented.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
