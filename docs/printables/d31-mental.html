Mental math warm-up: equal groups, and a comparison

Unit 7 · Day 31 · Ratio as a comparison · one-lesson review

Remember

Equal groups can be counted. 2 + 3 = 5 parts in a 2 : 3 comparison. Dividing both numbers by the same number keeps the comparison the same, which we will teach in the core.

1. How many equal parts are there in a comparison of 4 and 5?

2. Share 20 into 4 equal groups. How many in one group?

3. A 2-to-3 comparison has how many parts in all?

4. Share 18 into 3 equal groups. How many in two of those groups?
