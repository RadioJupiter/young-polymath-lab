Math circle: sharing equally, and a ‘fair’ cut

Unit 3 · Day 11 · The Moon's quarters · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Equal parts make a fraction honest. A ‘half’ that is not equal to the other half is not 1/2.

1. Three people share a ribbon equally. What fraction does each get? Shade a bar to show it.

2. A pizza is cut into 4 parts that are not equal, and someone takes the largest part and calls it 1/4. What would you say?

3. Draw two different ways to cut a rectangle into fourths (four equal parts).

4. A bar is 12 units long. A piece 3 units long is what fraction of the bar? A piece 4 units long?

5. Why does ‘I broke it into four pieces’ not automatically mean each piece is 1/4?

6. Write a two-sentence argument: This is 1/2 because ________. It would not be 1/2 if ________.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
