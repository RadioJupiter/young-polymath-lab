Challenge problems: mixed polygons, and a pad that is not a crater

Unit 5 · Day 22 · Polygons, and the angles of a triangle · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Triangle 180°. n-gon sum (n − 2) × 180°. Regular means equal sides and equal interior angles. Invented pads labeled. Crater rims are not polygons.

1. A five-sided invented courtyard is not regular. Its interior angles are 100°, 110°, 120°, 90°, and one missing. Find the missing angle.

2. Why is “the crater is a regular hexagon 120° at each corner” not an honest sentence in this course?

3. An isosceles triangle has two equal angles of 70°. Find the third. Is the triangle also equilateral?

4. A regular decagon (10 sides): each interior angle.

5. Write one sentence that could head a design page: invented polygon listed, crater not claimed as a polygon.
