Core lesson: percent as per hundred

Unit 7 · Day 34 · Percent as per hundred · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Percent means per hundred. 25% = 25/100 = 1/4 = 0.25. 50% is half, 25% is a quarter, 10% is a tenth, 5% is half of 10%. A percent of a quantity needs a named whole. Invented collections stay labeled. We will not invent a NASA percent.

1. Write 50% as a fraction in lowest terms, and find 50% of 90.

2. What is 10% of 250, and what is 5% of 250?

3. Find 30% of 80 (find 10% first).

4. An invented pack has 200 cards, and 40 are marked. What percent are marked?

5. Why is “25% is 25” not a complete answer to “what is 25% of 40?”

6. Write 0.4 as a percent. (0.4 is 40 hundredths.)
