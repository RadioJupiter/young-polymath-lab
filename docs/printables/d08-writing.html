Copy the model. Then finish A full Moon happens because / but / so.

Model sentence (copy on the line below):

The Moon reflects sunlight.

Your sentences:

A full Moon happens when ________.

My predicted date for the next full Moon is ________.

A full Moon happens because ________.

A full Moon happens but ________.

A full Moon happens so ________.

For the guide: Help the student shade the unlit half first, then ask which position looks full from Earth. Do not answer first. After the draft is down, tidy spelling from the word bank. Do not correct every error in the first writing.
