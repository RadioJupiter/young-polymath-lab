Unit 1 · Day 3 · Adding up the worlds

Notebook project · Polymath Lab · d03 · notebook

NameDate

Notebook · How big is the Sun?

1. Draw first. Draw Earth as a 1 cm circle, Jupiter at the same scale (about 11.2 cm across), and a piece of the Sun's edge. Write how wide the whole Sun would be (109 cm).

2. Label from the word bank. Point, say the word, then write it on the drawing.

Word bank — Earth · Jupiter · Sun · diameter · scale · centimetre · wider · curve · huge · edge

3. Write. Say each sentence aloud, then write it on the lines.

Earth is drawn ________.

Jupiter is about ________ times as wide as Earth.

The whole Sun at this scale would be ________.

Finish all three, with the same opening:

The Sun is huge because ________.

The Sun is huge but ________.

The Sun is huge so ________.

4. Numbers and questions. Check: is 11 × 12,756 close to 142,984? Is 109 × 12,756 close to 1,391,400? Show your rounding or working.

For the guide: New step: because / but / so. Ask 'because why?' rather than dictating the ending. The student speaks each full sentence first.
