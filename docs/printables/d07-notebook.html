Notebook · A clock for Mars

1. Draw first. Invent and draw a clock face or calendar for people living on Mars. Label the hours, and any extra time you include.

2. Label from the word bank. Point, say the word, then write it on the drawing.

Word bank — Mars · sol · hour · minute · clock · calendar · Earth time · extra · advantage · problem

3. Write. Say each sentence aloud, then write it on the lines.

My Mars clock ________.

One advantage is ________.

One problem is ________.

4. Numbers and questions. After 3 sols, how far behind Earth time is a Mars-time worker? After 9 sols? Show the working.

For the guide: The design must be the student's. Oral rehearsal of the three sentences first. Number them 1, 2, 3 if that helps.
