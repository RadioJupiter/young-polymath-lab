Remember

A Level 6 argument uses a bar or a renaming step, and says what the claim is not.

1. Which claim did you pick, and what picture or renaming did you use?

2. What false step did you rule out?

3. Write the argument in four to six sentences, with a bar or a renaming you actually drew.

4. Exchange with the guide: can they follow the reason without guessing what you meant?

5. If one part of today’s unit check was missed, write one new item like it and solve it here. (This is review, not a second score.)

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
