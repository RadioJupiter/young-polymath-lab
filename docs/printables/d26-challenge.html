Remember

Same places as the core. Invented lengths stay labeled. Do not invent a decimal NASA measurement.

1. Write 13 tenths as a decimal greater than 1, and as ones and tenths.

2. Is 0.25 the same amount as 1/4? Say why, in hundredths.

3. Write 7 hundredths and 7 tenths, and compare them.

4. A hundred-square has 40 small squares shaded. Write that as a decimal and as tenths.

5. Why will this course not write “Mars is 6,792.35 km across” as a NASA fact from this lesson?

6. 3.14 × 10 = 31.4. In 31.4, what does the 4 stand for now?
