Challenge problems: mixed units, and a comparison with Earth

Unit 4 · Day 16 · Meters, centimeters, and a planet's size · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Mixed units name one length. Regroup 100 cm as 1 m. Keep NASA comparisons labeled, and do not invent a new Mars figure.

1. Subtract 1 m 80 cm from 3 m 10 cm.

2. A model rover path in a course problem (invented, labeled) is 2 km 500 m. Write it in meters, then say how that invented path compares with Mars’s real diameter (it should be tiny).

3. 4 m 90 cm + 30 cm, in meters and centimeters.

4. Order from shortest to longest: 2,000 mm, 1.5 m, 180 cm. (Use one unit.)

5. Half of 6,792 km is how many kilometers? (That is a number fact, not a claim that a “half-Mars” exists.)

6. Why must “about 6,800 km” be labeled as rounded if you use it in a sentence with 12,756 km?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
