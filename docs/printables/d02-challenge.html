Remember

To the nearest thousand, the numbers that become N000 run from N000 − 500 up to (but not including) N000 + 500.

1. What is the largest whole number that rounds to 380,000 to the nearest ten thousand?

2. A secret number rounds to 380,000 (to the nearest ten thousand) and to 400,000 (to the nearest hundred thousand). What could it be? Is the Moon's distance a possibility?

3. What is the smallest whole number that rounds to 13,000 (nearest thousand)? The largest?

4. About how many times farther from the Sun is Jupiter than Earth? Use rounded numbers and explain.
