Remember

The three angles of a triangle add to 180°. A right angle is 90°. A straight angle is 180°.

1. A triangle has angles 70° and 50°. Find the third.

2. A triangle has two right angles. Is that possible? Why?

3. What is 180° − 90° − 30°?

4. Three angles 60°, 60°, and 60° make what kind of triangle, if the sides also match?
