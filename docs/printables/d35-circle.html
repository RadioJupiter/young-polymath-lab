Math circle: a last short argument

Unit 7 · Day 35 · Level 10 check · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Name the whole, name the scale, name the source. Invented mixes and walks stay invented. This circle is not a second score.

1. Name one scale from Day 5 and one scale from this unit, and one job for each.

2. Why is a percent without a named whole not yet a quantity?

3. Are “about 28,000 km/h” and “five miles per second” two votes to average?

4. What is 42,000 km (28,000 × 1.5) allowed to be, and what is it not?

5. Why is a printed star chart usually the wrong place to write a cm-to-kilometer scale?

6. How does 25% sit beside 0.25 and 1/4, and why is that not the same as writing “25”?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
