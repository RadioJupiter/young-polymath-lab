Remember

Equivalent ratios scale both sides by the same factor. A scale is a ratio: 1 unit on the drawing stands for a stated real length; every length uses the same scale. At 1 cm : 1,000 km, Earth is about 12.8 cm across, the Moon about 3.5 cm, and the gap about 3.84 m, too wide for the page. A star chart is usually angles, not a distance scale. NASA: the Sun’s diameter is about 400 times the Moon’s, and the Moon is roughly 400 times closer; our figures give about 400 and about 390, and we will not force them to match.
