Placement check

Do this with your guide over one or two sittings. It is not a test to pass: it finds where to start. Work section by section. Stop after any section with two or more answers wrong, or when the work stops feeling possible. Show working on paper. Then use the placement answer key.

Level 1

  1. 8 + 7
  2. 13 − 6
  3. 46 + 30
  4. What is 10 less than 300?

Level 2

  1. Write 4,036 as thousands, hundreds, tens and ones.
  2. 285 + 347
  3. 1,000 − 264
  4. Rocket A is 63 m tall. Rocket B is 18 m shorter. How tall is Rocket B? How tall are they together if stacked?

Level 3

  1. 7 × 8
  2. 54 ÷ 6
  3. How many in 4 rows of 9?
  4. Three bags hold 24 rocks shared equally. How many rocks are in 5 such bags?

Level 4

  1. Write 3,050,400 in words.
  2. Round 46,750 to the nearest thousand.
  3. 38,406 + 27,895
  4. 50,000 − 18,763

Level 5

  1. 326 × 7
  2. 48 × 25
  3. 918 ÷ 6
  4. How many seconds are in 15 minutes?

Level 6

  1. Draw a rectangle and shade 3/4 of it.
  2. Which is larger, 2/3 or 3/5? Explain.
  3. 1/4 + 2/4
  4. What is 1/6 of 54?

Level 7

  1. Find the perimeter of a rectangle 7 cm by 4 cm.
  2. Find its area.
  3. How many centimetres are in 3 metres?
  4. How many 1 cm cubes fill a box 3 cm by 4 cm by 5 cm?

Level 8

  1. What do the three angles of a triangle add up to?
  2. How many degrees are in a right angle?
  3. A circle has radius 6 cm. What is its diameter?
  4. How many lines of symmetry does a square have?

Problem-solving readiness (everyone tries these)

  1. How many squares of any size are there in a 3 × 3 grid?
  2. I am a two-digit number. My digits add up to 9, and I divide exactly by 4. What could I be? Find every answer.
  3. Four friends each shake hands once with each other. How many handshakes?
